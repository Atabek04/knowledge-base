# Arabic Learning Market

## Intro & Framing

**Greeting:**
"Thanks so much for taking the time. I really appreciate it."

**Framing:**
We're exploring something in the Arabic learning space. 
I'm mostly trying to understand your experience
no pitch, no selling. 
Just listening

**Expectation-setting:**
"I'll ask questions, you'll do most of the talking. Sound good?"

**Time:**
"Should take about 20-30 minutes."

---

## Discovery Phase

**Opening:**
Tell me about your relationship with Arabic right now.

**Branch A (Currently learning):**
How long have you been learning?

What are you using to learn?

What frustrates you most about it?

**Branch B (Not learning / stopped):**
What would it take for you to start learning Arabic?

What have you tried in the past?

Why did you stop?

---

## Behavior & Currency

How much time per week do you spend on Arabic right now?

What did you pay for your last Arabic learning method?

How long did you use it before stopping?

---

## Competitors & Pricing

Have you bought any paid Arabic courses?

Why did you choose that over [Zoom classes / YouTube / other option]?

Have you heard of or tried Arabicle.ru?

Why didn't you stick with it? (if they tried)

What's stopping you from using more paid courses right now?

---

## AI & Gamification Experience

Have you used Duolingo or similar gamified language apps?

Which ones? How long did you use each?

What kept you going, and why did you stop?

---

Have you tried any AI tools for practicing Arabic? (ChatGPT, language apps with AI, etc.)

How did you use it? How often?

What was frustrating about it?

---

## Switching Costs & Job to Be Done

What's your ultimate goal with Arabic? (Quran reading? Classical texts? Speaking?)

What would convince you to switch to something new?
