# Interview Scripts by Segment — Printable Reference

Quick-reference scripts for conducting Mom Test interviews with each customer segment.

**How to use:** Print the script for your target segment before interviews. Check off questions as you ask them. Write notes directly on the script.

---

## SEGMENT 1: Telegram Live Class Students

**Interview Goal:** Validate that they'll pay $20/month for hybrid model (pre-recorded + live) vs free Telegram groups.

**Interview Length:** 25-30 minutes
**Recording:** Request permission at start

---

### Opening (2 min) [✓ READ VERBATIM]

*"Thanks so much for taking time to chat. We're doing research with people learning Arabic to understand their current approach and what's working/not working. I'm not here to pitch anything — I'm just listening to understand your experience better. This should take about 25 minutes. Does that work?"*

---

### Current Solution Discovery (5 min)

**Question 1:**
"Tell me about the Telegram classes you're taking."

*Note what you learn:*
- [ ] How many per week?
- [ ] How large is the group?
- [ ] What's the teacher like?
- [ ] What are they learning?
- [ ] How long have they been at it?

---

**Question 2:**
"How did you find this Telegram group?"

*Note:*
- [ ] Friend recommendation?
- [ ] Google search?
- [ ] Facebook group?
- [ ] Other?

---

**Question 3:**
"How much do you pay for these classes?"

*Note: EXACT AMOUNT*
- [ ] Free
- [ ] Per class: $___
- [ ] Monthly: $___
- [ ] Other arrangement: ___

---

**Question 4:**
"How many times per week do you attend live classes?"

*Note: FREQUENCY*
- [ ] Times per week: ___
- [ ] Does this vary?
- [ ] When do they typically study?

---

**Question 5:**
"Show me your Telegram group — what does a typical class look like?"

*Note: OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOR*
- [ ] Group size (count members or get rough estimate)
- [ ] Message volume (active or quiet?)
- [ ] What language? (Arabic? Russian? Mixed?)
- [ ] What's the current activity?
- [ ] How engaged do students seem?

---

### Pain Point Discovery (7 min)

**Question 6:**
"Tell me about the last class you attended. Walk me through exactly what happened."

*Note: SPECIFIC RECENT BEHAVIOR*
- [ ] When was it? How long ago?
- [ ] What was taught?
- [ ] How long did it last?
- [ ] How engaged were they?
- [ ] Mood/tone when describing?

---

**Question 7:**
"What happens when you miss a class?"

*Note: FLEXIBILITY PAIN*
- [ ] Do they watch recording? (Can they?)
- [ ] Do they catch up on their own?
- [ ] Do they just miss it?
- [ ] How often do they miss?
- [ ] Does it bother them?

---

**Question 8:**
"Have you ever wanted to review something from a previous class? What did you do?"

*Note: SELF-PACED NEED*
- [ ] Specific example?
- [ ] How did they solve it?
- [ ] Did they ask teacher?

- [ ] How often does this happen?

**Question 9:**
