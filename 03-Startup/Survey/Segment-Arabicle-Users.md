# Segment 2: Arabicle.ru Platform Users

Conditional survey questions for respondents currently using or who have used Arabicle.ru platform.

**Segment Goal:** Understand what attracted them to Arabicle, why they're not actively using it (churn signals), what pain points a new competitor could address.

**Critical Insight:** Arabicle users represent a churned or at-risk segment that paid once ($48.97); understanding why they left is gold for product-market fit.

**Interview Goal:** Validate assumptions about repetition-based learning limitations, explore unmet needs that drove churn, identify what would reactivate them.

---

## Q10: How long have you been using Arabicle?
**Type:** Multiple choice (single select)

**Options:**
- Less than 1 month
- 1-3 months
- 3-6 months
- 6-12 months
- More than 1 year
- I stopped using it

**Purpose:** Retention timeline; shows if they're current users or churned

**Analysis:**
- "Less than 1 month" = very new; may reactivate
- "I stopped using it" = churned; **PRIORITY for interview** (understand why)
- "6-12 months" = sticky; either satisfied or in denial
- "More than 1 year" = highly committed; validate what works

---

## Q11: What did you pay for Arabicle?
**Type:** Short answer (numeric)

**Purpose:** Confirm sunk cost ($48.97 expected); validate pricing anchor

**Red flag:** Amount vastly different from $48.97 → may have bundle/promo; note for context

## Q12: What made you choose Arabicle initially?
**Type:** Checkboxes (multiple select)

**Options:**
- [ ] Friend recommendation
- [ ] Found it online (ad, search, blog post)
- [ ] Liked the structured approach (grammar rules)
- [ ] One-time payment (not subscription)
- [ ] Saw positive reviews
- [ ] Wanted alternatives to free resources
- [ ] Appeals to Islamic learners
- [ ] Other (please specify)

**Purpose:** Acquisition channel + initial value perception; shows what attracted them

**Analysis:**
- "One-time payment" = price-sensitive; single payment felt lower risk
- "Structured approach" = value in curriculum; content organization matters
- "Friend recommendation" = word-of-mouth works; testimonial weight

**Interview follow-up:** "You mentioned [reason]. Walk me through how you first heard about Arabicle."

## Q13: What FRUSTRATES you about Arabicle?
**Type:** Checkboxes (multiple select)

**Options:**
- [ ] Repetition-based learning feels boring
- [ ] No grammar teaching/explanation (just repetition)
- [ ] Progress feels slow
- [ ] Interface is outdated/ugly
- [ ] No speaking practice (only written)
- [ ] Feels isolated (no community, no teacher)
- [ ] All content is prescriptive (can't choose path)
- [ ] Doesn't connect to real Arabic texts (Quran, classical)
- [ ] No accountability (can pause forever)
- [ ] One-time payment means no updates/support
- [ ] Other (please specify)

**Purpose:** Identify pain points that new platform could solve

**Critical pains:**
- "Boring repetition" = highest-impact churn driver for Arabicle specifically
- "No grammar" = teachable moment (competitors teach rules)
- "Isolated" = community opportunity
- "No speaking" = engagement issue

**Interview follow-up:** "You said [frustration]. Tell me about when you realized this was a problem."

## Q14: Are you still actively using Arabicle? *(CRITICAL)*
**Type:** Multiple choice (single select) — *CONDITIONAL BRANCHING*

**Options:**
- [ ] Yes, I use it regularly (3+ times per week) → SKIP TO Q16
- [ ] Yes, but only occasionally (1-2 times per week) → GO TO Q15
- [ ] No, I stopped using it → GO TO Q15
- [ ] I haven't finished it yet (still progressing) → SKIP TO Q16

**Purpose:** Retention status; determines if user is active, at-risk, or churned

**Red flags:**
- "Stopped using" = churned; **INTERVIEW PRIORITY: HIGH**
- "Occasionally" = at-risk churn; needs reactivation

**Type:** Linear scale (1-5)

**Scale:**
- 1 = Very unsatisfied

## Interview Script (After Survey Match)

When interviewing Segment 2 respondents, prioritize these areas:

1. **Initial purchase decision** (3-5 min)
   - "When you bought Arabicle for $48.97, what problem were you trying to solve?"
   - "What other options did you consider before choosing Arabicle?"
   - "What convinced you to spend money vs using free resources?"

2. **Usage pattern** (5-7 min)
   - "Walk me through your typical study session with Arabicle. What did you do first?"
   - "How often did you study? Was that your goal or did life get in the way?"
   - "Show me the last lesson you completed — what was it?"

3. **Churn moment** (5-10 min) — *IF CHURNED*
   - "When did you stop using Arabicle? Do you remember the exact moment?"
   - "What were you using instead?"
   - "Have you thought about going back?"
   - "What would need to change for you to try it again?"

4. **What worked/didn't** (5-7 min)
   - "What part of Arabicle actually helped you learn?"
   - "What was the biggest frustration?"
   - "You mentioned [frustration]. How did that make you feel about continuing?"

5. **Alternative methods** (3-5 min)
   - "You mentioned using [YouTube/ChatGPT/live classes/etc]. How is that working?"
   - "What's better about it than Arabicle?"
   - "What do you miss about Arabicle?"

6. **Reactivation potential** (2-3 min)
   - "If Arabicle solved [key frustration], would you go back?"
   - "What would be a fair price for an improved version?"

## Competitive Positioning Insights

If you interview 10 Arabicle users:

- Count how many stopped due to "boring repetition" — if 7/10, that's product validation gold
- Track "found another method" mentions — what methods are they switching to?
- Note who stayed beyond 6 months — what made them stick?
- Identify unmet needs mentioned in "Other" frustrations — competitive opportunities
