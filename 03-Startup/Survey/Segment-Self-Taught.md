# Segment 3: Self-Taught Learners

Conditional survey questions for respondents learning Arabic independently using free resources (YouTube, ChatGPT, textbooks, etc.).

**Segment Goal:** Understand why they choose free over paid, what barriers prevent payment, what would trigger willingness to pay, validate pricing sensitivity.

**Critical Insight:** Self-taught learners represent the largest potential market ($0 → $20-30/month conversion) but are often overlooked because they're not "customers" yet.

**Interview Goal:** Identify what support they're missing, what they'd pay for, what's preventing trial of paid methods.

---

## Q10: What free resources are you using RIGHT NOW?
**Type:** Checkboxes (multiple select)

**Options:**
- [ ] YouTube videos/channels
- [ ] ChatGPT or AI tools (Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.)
- [ ] Free textbooks/PDFs
- [ ] Language exchange partners
- [ ] Free apps (Duolingo, AnkiDroid, etc.)
- [ ] Online forums/communities (Reddit, Discord)
- [ ] Podcast/audio (YouTube Music, Spotify)
- [ ] Native speakers/friends helping
- [ ] Other (please specify)

**Purpose:** Understand current substitutes; what are they getting from free resources

**Analysis:**
- "YouTube" + "ChatGPT" = mixing high-quality content with generative AI (sophisticated learner)
- "Duolingo" only = gamification-dependent learner
- "Language exchange" = social/accountability motivated
- Multiple sources = bootstrapping their own curriculum

**Interview follow-up:** "You mentioned [resource]. Walk me through how you use that in a typical study session."

---

## Q11: How many hours per week do you study Arabic?
**Type:** Multiple choice (single select)

**Options:**
- Less than 1 hour
- 1-3 hours
- 3-5 hours
- 5-10 hours
- More than 10 hours

**Purpose:** Commitment level despite lack of structure; self-directed learners often self-select for discipline

**Analysis:**
- 10+ hours = highly motivated; would likely pay for structure
- 5-10 hours = serious learner; good interview candidate
- 1-3 hours = casual; may not have budget for paid learning
- <1 hour = exploring; low conversion probability

---

## Q12: What's your BIGGEST challenge with self-teaching?
**Type:** Paragraph (open-ended) — *CRITICAL INSIGHT*

**Purpose:** Unprompted pain point discovery; more authentic than checkbox options

**Example answers we're looking for:**
- "I don't know if I'm learning grammar correctly. YouTube doesn't explain why."
- "I have no way to practice speaking without a partner."
- "I feel like I'm wasting time; there's no clear path forward."
- "I learn a bunch of random vocab but can't connect it into sentences."

**Interview follow-up:** "You said [challenge]. Tell me about the last time that frustrated you. What happened?"

---

## Q13: Have you tried PAID methods before? *(CRITICAL)*
**Type:** Multiple choice (single select) — *CONDITIONAL BRANCHING*

**Options:**
- [ ] Yes, I paid for courses/apps before → GO TO Q14
- [ ] No, I've only used free resources → SKIP TO Q15
- [ ] I tried free trials but didn't pay → GO TO Q14

**Purpose:** Willingness-to-pay history; shows if they've overcome psychological barrier to payment

**Analysis:**
- "Yes" = price-accepting; understand what they tried and why they stopped
- "No" = price-resistant or budget-constrained; learn barriers to conversion
- "Free trials" = interested in paid but something blocked them; highest-value interview

---

## Q14: What paid methods did you try? Why did you stop? *(conditional on Q13)*
**Type:** Paragraph (open-ended)

**Purpose:** Competitive analysis + understand churn from paid services

**Example answers:**
- "I tried Babbel for 2 months, but it was too expensive at $15/month and not specific to Arabic."
- "I bought an Udemy course, but it was too fast and there was nowhere to ask questions."
- "I tried Arabicle, but the repetition got boring after a month."

**Interview follow-up:** "You paid for [method]. What made you decide to try it? And what was the moment you realized it wasn't working?"

---

## Q15: What would make you consider PAYING for Arabic learning?
**Type:** Checkboxes (multiple select)

**Options:**
- [ ] Structured curriculum (clear learning path from beginner → advanced)
- [ ] Progress tracking (see visible progress)
- [ ] Grammar explanations (understand the "why," not just memorize)
- [ ] Speaking practice (pronunciation feedback, conversation partners)
- [ ] Community/accountability (group of learners, accountability partner)
- [ ] Certificate/credential (proof of proficiency)
- [ ] Personalization (learn what I want to learn)
- [ ] Quality teachers (not just videos, real interaction)
- [ ] Nothing (I prefer free resources)
- [ ] Other (please specify)

**Purpose:** Value perception; identify which features would trigger payment

**Analysis:**
- "Structured curriculum" + "Progress tracking" = most common (learners want direction)
- "Speaking practice" = rarely available in free tier; high-value differentiator
- "Community" = motivation driver; social learning important
- "Nothing" = price-resistant; likely not conversion candidate

**Interview follow-up:** "You said you'd consider paying for [feature]. Why is that important to you?"

---

## Q16: If you found a great Arabic learning platform, how much would you be willing to pay per month?
**Type:** Multiple choice (single select)

**Purpose:** Pricing sensitivity; identify acceptable price band

**Options:**
- $0 (only free)
- $5-10
- $10-20
- $20-30
- $30-40
- More than $40

**Important:** This question asks about an existing platform at familiar price points (not hypothetical "would you pay $X?"). Anchors to known markets (Duolingo ~$5-15, Babbel ~$15, live classes $25-40).

**Analysis:**
- "$20-30" = ideal for competitive positioning against live classes
- "$10-20" = price-conscious; concern about affordability
- "$30-40" = willing to pay premium; signals high value perception
- "$0" = not a realistic conversion candidate

**Interview follow-up:** "You said $[amount]. At what price would it feel too expensive?"

---

## Q17: On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with self-teaching?
**Type:** Linear scale (1-5)

**Scale:**
- 1 = Very unsatisfied (spinning wheels, no progress)
- 2 = Somewhat unsatisfied (making progress but with major gaps)
- 3 = Neutral (it works okay, but not ideal)
- 4 = Somewhat satisfied (good progress despite challenges)
- 5 = Very satisfied (ideal setup for me)

**Purpose:** Baseline satisfaction; understand if they'd actually switch

**Analysis:**
- 1-2 = frustrated; high conversion potential
- 3 = acceptable but not ideal; pricing becomes lever
- 4-5 = content with current approach; hard to convert unless pain point specific

## Interview Script (After Survey Match)

When interviewing Segment 3 respondents, prioritize these areas:

1. **Current setup & workflow** (5-7 min)
   - "Walk me through your typical study session. What resources do you use?"
   - "Show me your setup — what's on your desk/screen right now?"
   - "How often do you study? What time of day?"

2. **Self-taught learning journey** (5-10 min)
   - "Why did you choose to self-teach instead of paying for classes?"
   - "Have you tried paid methods before? [If yes] Why did you go back to free?"
   - "What made you choose [YouTube/ChatGPT/textbooks]? What works about them?"

3. **Biggest pain point** (7-10 min) — *DEEPEST DIVE*
   - "You mentioned [Q12 answer] as your biggest challenge. Tell me about the last time that happened."
   - "How does that make you feel about continuing?"
   - "What would fix that problem?"
   - "How much effort have you spent on [workaround]?"

4. **Progress visibility** (3-5 min)
   - "How do you know you're actually improving?"
   - "Show me — how do you measure progress?"
   - "Do you ever feel lost or unsure if you're learning the right things?"

5. **What would trigger payment** (5-7 min)
   - "You said you'd consider paying for [Q15 answer]. Tell me about that."
   - "What's stopping you from paying for that right now?"
   - "If it cost $[price from Q16], would you try it?"

6. **Switching scenario** (3-5 min)
   - "Imagine you found a paid platform that solved [pain point]. What would need to be true for you to try it?"
   - "Would your friends/learning partners try it with you?"
   - "What would make you trust it wasn't wasting your money?"

## Common Segment 3 Patterns

| Survey Response | Interpretation | Interview Priority |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| YouTube + ChatGPT + 5+ hours/week | Sophisticated bootstrapper | **PRIORITY** — quality validation |
| "No structure" pain + willing $20-30 | Perfect conversion target | **PRIORITY** |
| Tried paid before + quit | Specific pain point | Identify deal-breakers |
| 1-3 hours/week + $0 price | Low commitment + budget | Low conversion probability |
| "Community" as top need + isolated setup | Unmmet social motivation | High switching cost if community provided |
| 10+ hours/week + frustrated | Highly motivated, seeking better tools | **PRIORITY** |

## Competitive Positioning Opportunities

If you interview 15 self-taught learners:

- **Identify most common pain points** (from Q12) — what % mention structure? speaking? community?
- **Track payment conversion readiness** — How many went from "$0" to "willing to pay"?
- **Understand resource stacking** — Do most use YouTube + ChatGPT together? Validate substitutability
- **Validate price band** — Cluster responses around $10-20 vs $20-30 vs $30-40
- **Identify switching triggers** — What would actually make them buy?
