Every resource evaluated for one candidate profile: Academic, targeting 8.0, currently somewhere around 6.5–7.0, ~6 h/week, computer-delivered, Astana, February 2027. A resource that is excellent for a 5.5 → 6.5 candidate and useless at 7 is marked as such and cut.

All prices captured **4 September 2026** and will drift. Ranked within each section.

Two structural facts govern the whole table, and both are recent:

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">IELTS Progress Check was discontinued in June 2024.</mark> It was the only practice test marked by trained IELTS markers with per-section band output. There is now **no route, free or paid, to an official human-examiner band score outside a real sitting**. Every band number in the table below is either an AI estimate or a raw-score conversion from an answer key.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The best free resource is gated on booking the test.</mark> British Council's IELTS Ready Premium unlocks on booking, not on payment — see [[Booking the test unlocks the best free IELTS resource so booking comes before diagnosing]].

## Speaking

The weakest part of the ecosystem. <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">No free tool gives a trustworthy speaking band.</mark> The "95% accurate" figures these vendors publish have no published validation against human examiners.

| Resource | Free/Paid | What it's for | Verdict | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keith Speaking Academy YouTube** ("English Speaking Success") | Free | Part 2/3 method, fluency under pressure, idiomatic lexis at band 7+ | **Best free speaking teaching online**, and one of very few channels that teaches above band 7 rather than explaining the format | [keithspeakingacademy.com](https://keithspeakingacademy.com/) |
| Keith **Interactive tier** | $150, lifetime | 2 live lessons/month + peer practice rooms | **Buy only if you will actually attend the live rooms.** The product is forced production, not more video — that is the part you cannot self-supply | [keithspeakingacademy.com](https://keithspeakingacademy.com/ielts-speaking-online-courses/) |
| Self-recording + descriptor self-audit | Free | Transcribe, then mark yourself against the official descriptors | **Underrated and evidence-aligned.** At 7→8 the deciding criteria are lexical resource and grammatical range, both auditable from a transcript far better than any free AI manages | [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) |
| Shadowing short clips | Free | Prosody: rhythm, weak forms, sentence stress | **The practice method with real research behind it** — improves prosody and fluency; inconclusive on segmentals, which you should deprioritise anyway | see [[Prosodic pronunciation training transfers to spontaneous speech while segmental training does not]] |
| SmallTalk2Me / AI4IELTS / PrepAsk | Free tiers | AI band estimate across the four criteria | **Directional signal only.** Two tools agreeing at 6.5 is signal; one saying 8.0 is noise | [smalltalk2.me](https://smalltalk2.me/ielts) |
| Engnovate speaking evaluator | Free tier | AI speaking band | **Distrust.** A reviewer reports it "always gives 7" — including to someone who then scored 5.5 | [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/engnovate.com) |

## Skip list — with the reason, not just the verdict

| Resource | Price | Why skip |
|---|---|---|
| **IELTS Advantage VIP Academy** | $497 | **No independent review footprint at all** — no Trustpilot profile, zero third-party reviews, testimonials self-hosted only. ~$70 per human touchpoint for 7 touchpoints you can buy for ~$150 à la carte. The "Band 8+ in all four" refund clause is harder than the target itself |
| **E2 Gold** | $394.90 | Two and a half exam sittings for a video library you don't need at band 7 |
| **Magoosh IELTS** | $129/6mo | Built for a 5.5→6.5 audience; volume over precision. The 7-day refund is **voided the moment you use the grading** — the one feature worth having |
| **Fastrack IELTS** | $70 | Its own FAQ states it includes **no writing or speaking assessment**. At band 7, ungraded content is the part you least need |
| **TestGlider** | Unverifiable | Price behind a login wall, no usable review base, and free mock simulation exists elsewhere |
| **Engnovate subscription** | $11.97–$61.97 | Free tier is the useful part. Reviews report an unauthorised third-party charge attempt |
| **E2 / Fastrack / IELTS Advantage free video courses** | Free | Formulaic template teaching — the exact habit that caps Lexical Resource and Coherence at 6.5–7.0. Actively counterproductive at this target |
| **Cambridge IELTS 1–8** | — | Wrong difficulty calibration; inflates your Reading band |
| **Cambridge Write & Improve as a band estimator** | — | CEFR output, not IELTS bands. Cannot resolve 7.0 from 8.0, which is the only resolution that matters here |
| **IELTS review/comparison blogs** | Free | ieltsbiz, ielts.international, ieltsmaterial, ielts.net and similar are affiliate content — occasionally accurate because they quote first-party pages, never independent |

Total avoided by the skip list: roughly **$1,400**.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Test fee, Astana (110,250₸, both providers) | ~$241 |

| Write & Improve + Test Zone, 5 months | ~$30 |

| IELTS Ready Premium, descriptors, Keith's YouTube, Engnovate free tier | $0 |
| **Total excluding the sitting** | **$240–470** |

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The exam fee is itself a purchase in this comparison.</mark> If marked practice clusters at 7.5+ by December, a second sitting is a better use of 110,250₸ than any course above — and a One Skill Retake, at 69,500₸ through the British Council, is cheaper still if only Writing lags. ## Listening: dictation, spelling and typing tools

Verified September 2026. These exist to serve one loop — produce spelling errors, then re-drill your own, per [[Dictation with immediate comparison is the spelling remediation that matches the Listening task|the remediation note]]. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">A tool that does not either generate errors or re-drill logged ones earns no time.</mark>

| Tool | Cost | What it drills | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DailyDictation](https://dailydictation.com/exercises/ielts-listening) | Free; some lessons premium-gated, price not published | Type what you hear, auto-diff per word. Cambridge 3–21. Separate **Spelling Names** and **Numbers** decks | The engine — nothing else combines IELTS audio with automatic comparison |
| [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) + [HyperTTS](https://www.vocab.ai/hypertts) | Free (iOS app paid) | Audio-front, type-the-answer cards built **only from your own error log** | The only tool that guarantees a week-1 miss returns in week 5 |
| [Monkeytype](https://monkeytype.com) | Free | Custom-text mode — paste your error list. Accuracy target, never speed | Worth it only if mock triage shows *mistyped* rather than *misspelled* |
| [British Council CD familiarisation test](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare/free-ielts-english-practice-tests/ielts-on-computer/familiarisation-test) | Free | The real interface — answer boxes, volume slider, review flags | Do it twice before test day. It is **untimed**, so time it yourself |
| [IELTS Liz alphabet](https://ieltsliz.com/alphabet-practice-for-spelling/) · [city names](https://ieltsliz.com/spelling-practice-city-names/) · [addresses](https://ieltsliz.com/listening-practice-for-addresses/) | Free | Letter-by-letter spell-outs, a distinct sub-skill | One-off calibration in week 1, not a recurring slot |
| [Lexinglo](https://lexinglo.com/lessons/ielts-listening) | Not stated | Cambridge 3–20, segmented player | Backup if DailyDictation's paywall blocks the tests you want |

Explicitly not worth the budget: **Cambly** and **EnglishCentral** train speaking, not transcription, at real cost. **ESLFast** sits far below this level. **Listen & Write** duplicates DailyDictation with no IELTS audio and uncontrolled accents. **ELLLO** is excellent and free but fixes comprehension, which is the wrong problem. `dictationsdaily.com` is a dead domain.

<mark style="background: #FF9E9EA6;">Practise with spellcheck disabled.</mark> The exam software offers no spelling assistance, and a browser that quietly fixes *vegeterian* trains you on a crutch that will not be there.
