## Retrieval & Active Pause

*Passive viewing produces weak retention. These principles govern how and when to interrupt video for active recall.*

- [[Interpolated retrieval testing after each video segment produces larger retention gains than restudying the same content|Interpolated retrieval beats restudying (d=1.38)]]
- [[Forward testing effect shows that retrieving prior content frees working memory to encode new incoming material|Forward testing: recall primes encoding the next segment]]

## Curiosity & Learner Motivation

*How the emotional and neurological state of the learner shapes encoding before content even begins.*

- [[Curiosity gap openings trigger dopaminergic encoding that boosts retention of all subsequent content|Curiosity gaps trigger dopaminergic encoding]]

## Worked Examples & Scaffolding

*How to design the transition from guided instruction to independent practice.*

- [[Backward fading removes final worked example steps first to preserve context while building independent problem-solving skill|Backward fading: remove the final steps first]]
- [[Expertise reversal effect makes scaffolded instruction counterproductive once learners build sufficient schema|Expertise reversal: scaffolding hurts once schema forms]]
- [[Pre-training principle reduces working memory split-attention by teaching component names before the main lesson begins|Pre-training: teach component names first]]

## Bilingual & Script Design

*Layout principles for video that teaches through two languages simultaneously.*
