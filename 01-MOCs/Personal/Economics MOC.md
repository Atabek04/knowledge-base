Concepts for understanding money, value exchange, and financial systems. Covers the history and evolution of money from pre-metallic societies to modern paper currency.

## Fundamental Problem

- [[Scarcity forces economies to allocate limited resources among unlimited uses|Scarcity: limited resources, unlimited uses]]

## Early Solutions to Scarcity

- [[Scarcity emerged when resources became limited after paradise|Scarcity emerged after paradise]]
- [[Collective good works in small communities with shared effort|Collective good works in small communities]]
- [[Perpetual debt systems rely on ongoing reciprocal obligations|Perpetual debt: ongoing reciprocal obligations]]
- [[Scaling perpetual debt across large populations became impossible|Perpetual debt doesn't scale to large populations]]
- [[Money emerged to settle debts immediately with portable work representation|Money: settle debts now with portable value]]

## Nature of Money

- [[Money represents stored labor and effort as transferable value|Money: stored labor as transferable value]]
- [[Inflation occurs when more money is printed than goods or backing exist|Inflation: more money than goods or backing]]

## AI and the Future of Labor

The thesis: if money is a claim on labor, then making labor cheap reshapes what money and wealth mean.

### The post-AGI argument (theory)

- [[Wealth is fundamentally a claim on other people's labor|Wealth is a claim on others' labor]]
- [[AGI labor at near-zero cost drives human wages toward zero|Near-zero AGI labor drives wages to zero]]
- [[Post-AGI human work is priced at the cost of replacing it with compute|Human work priced at the cost of compute]]
- [[Jobs survive automation when they aren't worth the compute, not because humans are better|Jobs survive when not worth the compute]]
- [[The post-AGI demand paradox leaves cheap goods with too few buyers|Demand paradox: cheap goods, too few buyers]]
- [[Robotic abundance crashes goods prices while scarce land keeps its value|Robotic abundance crashes goods; land stays scarce]]

### Power and inequality

- [[Post-AGI power concentrates with the owners of compute, energy, and land|Power concentrates in compute, energy, land owners]]
- [[AI shifts labor's leverage from withholding work to withholding consumption|Labor's lever shifts to withholding consumption]]
- [[UBI is the leading proposal to sustain demand in a post-labor economy|UBI sustains demand in a post-labor economy]]

### What the 2026 data actually shows (evidence)

- [[AI's labor-market damage appears first in hiring, not wages|AI hits hiring first, not wages]]
- [[AI automates codified book-knowledge, exposing entry-level roles most|AI automates book-knowledge; entry-level hit first]]
- [[AI displaces workers where it automates and spares them where it augments|AI displaces where it automates, spares where it augments]]
- [[Humans keep their edge in physical, strategic, and social work|Humans keep the edge in physical/strategic/social work]]
- [[The career ladder is giving way to an AI-enabled career lattice|Career ladder → AI-enabled career lattice]]

### The skeptic's counter-case

The other side: much of the "AI apocalypse" coverage is hype, and the 2026 data shows no collapse.

- [[Vibe reporting conflates tech layoffs with AI to manufacture an apocalypse narrative|Vibe reporting conflates layoffs with AI]]
- [[Recent tech layoffs are pandemic over-hiring corrections, not AI automation|Tech layoffs = pandemic over-hiring, not AI]]
- [[AI CEOs have an incentive to overstate job replacement|AI CEOs overstate job replacement]]
- [[Technology diffuses along an S-curve, not a runaway exponential|Tech diffuses on an S-curve, not exponential]]
- [[Physical compute constraints cap how fast AI can automate industries|Compute constraints cap automation speed]]
- [[Treating AI as a normal technology enables accountability and measured regulation|AI as normal technology enables regulation]]

- [[Pre-metallic societies used rare portable items as money|Pre-metallic money: shells, teeth, beads]]
