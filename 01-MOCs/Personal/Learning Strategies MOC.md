## Language Learning

*How languages are acquired, how vocabulary is built, and what research says about the most effective techniques for learners and teachers.*

### Input & Acquisition Foundations

- [[Comprehensible input at i+1 requires graded exposure sequenced by difficulty to drive acquisition|Comprehensible input at i+1 drives acquisition]]
- [[High affective filter blocks language acquisition even when input is comprehensible|High affective filter blocks acquisition]] *(see Pedagogy section)*
- [[Noticing Hypothesis states that conscious attention to form is a prerequisite for converting input to intake|Noticing: attention to form turns input into intake]]
- [[Immersion accelerates acquisition only when input remains comprehensible and engagement stays active|Immersion works only when input stays comprehensible]]
- [[Input laddering sequences listening and reading materials by difficulty to maintain comprehensible i+1 throughout acquisition|Input laddering keeps materials at i+1]]

### Output & Interaction

- [[Output Hypothesis states that producing language forces learners to notice gaps that input alone cannot reveal|Output hypothesis: producing language reveals gaps]]
- [[Interactional Hypothesis explains why negotiated conversation accelerates acquisition beyond passive exposure|Interaction hypothesis: negotiation speeds acquisition]]
- [[Speaking from day one builds acquisition through output pressure and immediate feedback from interaction|Speak from day one: output + feedback]]

### Vocabulary Architecture

- [[Nation's vocabulary load research identifies word-frequency thresholds for unaided text comprehension|Nation: frequency thresholds for text comprehension]]
- [[Deliberate vocabulary study and incidental acquisition target different frequency bands in the lexicon|Deliberate vs incidental vocab by frequency band]]
- [[Teaching vocabulary in semantic sets creates interference that slows initial acquisition|Semantic-set teaching causes interference]]
- [[Etymology and morphological root awareness multiply vocabulary acquisition by making word families transparent|Roots and etymology make word families transparent]]

### Video & Subtitle Design for L2

- [[Same-language captions produce larger L2 acquisition gains than translated captions across proficiency levels|Same-language captions beat translated (g=0.56)]]
- [[Sequential L1-then-bilingual subtitle exposure balances comprehension scaffolding with target-language form encoding|L1-then-bilingual subtitles: meaning then form]]
- [[Spatial contiguity between L2 target and translation eliminates split-attention cost in bilingual slide design|Spatial contiguity: L2 over L1 gloss (g=0.63)]]

### Vocabulary Techniques

- [[Keyword mnemonic links a foreign word to a vivid mental image via a sound-alike bridge|Keyword mnemonic: a sound-alike image bridge]]
- [[Method of Loci encodes vocabulary by placing words as objects along a memorized spatial route|Method of Loci: words along a spatial route]]
- [[Spaced repetition schedules vocabulary review at expanding intervals matched to the forgetting curve|Spaced repetition matches the forgetting curve]]
- [[Sentence mining builds Anki cards from real input to preserve the contextual meaning that isolated words lose|Sentence mining: i+1 cards from real input]]
- [[Shadowing trains prosody and fluency by simultaneously vocalizing native-speaker audio|Shadowing trains prosody by vocalizing audio]]
