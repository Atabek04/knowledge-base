Concepts for understanding money, value exchange, and financial systems. Covers the history and evolution of money from pre-metallic societies to modern paper currency.

## Fundamental Problem

- [[Scarcity forces economies to allocate limited resources among unlimited uses]] — why economics exists

## Early Solutions to Scarcity

- [[Scarcity emerged when resources became limited after paradise]] — the historical emergence of the economic problem
- [[Collective good works in small communities with shared effort]] — tribal economies without formal money
- [[Perpetual debt systems rely on ongoing reciprocal obligations]] — trust-based credit in small groups
- [[Scaling perpetual debt across large populations became impossible]] — the breaking point for informal systems
- [[Money emerged to settle debts immediately with portable work representation]] — formal currency as scalability solution

## Nature of Money

- [[Money represents stored labor and effort as transferable value]] — value abstraction and portability
- [[Inflation occurs when more money is printed than goods or backing exist]] — trust collapse and purchasing power loss

## Origin Theories

- [[Barter theory attributes money's origin to solving inefficient direct exchange]] — solution to double coincidence of wants
- [[Money originated as a record of debt between people]] — credit theory of money

## Evolution of Currency

- [[Pre-metallic societies used rare portable items as money]] — shells, teeth, beads as early currency
- [[Precious metal weights became currency before standardization]] — gold and silver as measured value (Ancient Egypt)
- [[Dirham originated from Greek drachma through Persian transmission]] — silver coin from Greek-Persian trade
- [[Dinar originated from Latin denarius through Hellenistic transmission]] — gold coin from Roman-Islamic standardization
- [[First stamped coin appeared around 650 BC in Lydia]] — standardization through official stamps
- [[Standardized coins reduced friction but government dilution destroyed trust]] — Lydian innovation and debasement consequences
- [[Paper money solved the portability problem of metal coins]] — China's innovation and trust requirements
