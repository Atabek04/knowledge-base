Concepts for understanding money, value exchange, and financial systems. Covers the history and evolution of money from pre-metallic societies to modern paper currency.

## Fundamental Problem

- [[Scarcity forces economies to allocate limited resources among unlimited uses]] — why economics exists

## Early Solutions to Scarcity

- [[Scarcity emerged when resources became limited after paradise]] — the historical emergence of the economic problem
- [[Collective good works in small communities with shared effort]] — tribal economies without formal money
- [[Perpetual debt systems rely on ongoing reciprocal obligations]] — trust-based credit in small groups
- [[Scaling perpetual debt across large populations became impossible]] — the breaking point for informal systems
- [[Money emerged to settle debts immediately with portable work representation]] — formal currency as scalability solution

## Nature of Money

- [[Money represents stored labor and effort as transferable value]] — value abstraction and portability
- [[Inflation occurs when more money is printed than goods or backing exist]] — trust collapse and purchasing power loss

## AI and the Future of Labor

The thesis: if money is a claim on labor, then making labor cheap reshapes what money and wealth mean.

### The post-AGI argument (theory)

- [[Wealth is fundamentally a claim on other people's labor]] — the premise everything else builds on
- [[AGI labor at near-zero cost drives human wages toward zero]] — the wage-collapse model
- [[Post-AGI human work is priced at the cost of replacing it with compute]] — compute becomes the scarce resource
- [[Jobs survive automation when they aren't worth the compute, not because humans are better]] — Restrepo's bottleneck argument
- [[The post-AGI demand paradox leaves cheap goods with too few buyers]] — production without consumption
- [[Robotic abundance crashes goods prices while scarce land keeps its value]] — where scarcity migrates

### Power and inequality

- [[Post-AGI power concentrates with the owners of compute, energy, and land]] — the new inequality
- [[AI shifts labor's leverage from withholding work to withholding consumption]] — the worker's remaining lever
- [[UBI is the leading proposal to sustain demand in a post-labor economy]] — redistribution as demand policy

### What the 2026 data actually shows (evidence)

- [[AI's labor-market damage appears first in hiring, not wages]] — Stanford "canaries in the coal mine"
- [[AI automates codified book-knowledge, exposing entry-level roles most]] — why the young get hit first
- [[AI displaces workers where it automates and spares them where it augments]] — automation vs augmentation
- [[Humans keep their edge in physical, strategic, and social work]] — comparative advantage
- [[The career ladder is giving way to an AI-enabled career lattice]] — adaptation strategy

### The skeptic's counter-case

The other side: much of the "AI apocalypse" coverage is hype, and the 2026 data shows no collapse.

- [[Vibe reporting conflates tech layoffs with AI to manufacture an apocalypse narrative]] — media-literacy critique (Cal Newport)
- [[Recent tech layoffs are pandemic over-hiring corrections, not AI automation]] — the boring, better-supported explanation
- [[AI CEOs have an incentive to overstate job replacement]] — discount the conflicted forecaster
- [[Technology diffuses along an S-curve, not a runaway exponential]] — adoption, not invention, sets the pace
- [[Physical compute constraints cap how fast AI can automate industries]] — the hard physical ceiling
- [[Treating AI as a normal technology enables accountability and measured regulation]] — the "normal technology" framing + Fed/NY Fed consensus

## Origin Theories

- [[Barter theory attributes money's origin to solving inefficient direct exchange]] — solution to double coincidence of wants
- [[Money originated as a record of debt between people]] — credit theory of money

## Evolution of Currency

- [[Pre-metallic societies used rare portable items as money]] — shells, teeth, beads as early currency
- [[Precious metal weights became currency before standardization]] — gold and silver as measured value (Ancient Egypt)
- [[Dirham originated from Greek drachma through Persian transmission]] — silver coin from Greek-Persian trade
- [[Dinar originated from Latin denarius through Hellenistic transmission]] — gold coin from Roman-Islamic standardization
- [[First stamped coin appeared around 650 BC in Lydia]] — standardization through official stamps
- [[Standardized coins reduced friction but government dilution destroyed trust]] — Lydian innovation and debasement consequences
- [[Paper money solved the portability problem of metal coins]] — China's innovation and trust requirements
