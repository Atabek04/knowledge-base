### The insight

Players who already know the [[A checks-captures-threats scan before every chess move catches most blunders|blunder-check habit]] still hang pieces — because <mark style="background: yellow">they move too fast, not because they lack knowledge.</mark>

The blunder is usually a *behavioral* failure, not a gap in understanding.

---
### Why the checklist isn't enough

Knowing the checks-captures-threats scan does nothing if you play the first move that looks right.

<mark style="background: cyan">The real fix is the clock, not more theory:</mark> use your time, sit on your hands, and don't commit to your first idea.

This is also why [[Long time controls build calculation skill that blitz cannot|bullet stalls improvement]] — there is no time to run the scan at all.

---
### Practical guard

On every move, force a pause before touching a piece.
<mark style="background: green">"Slow down and actually run the scan" beats any cleverer study plan for stopping piece blunders.</mark>

<mark style="background: pink">If you blunder despite knowing the checklist, the problem is speed and discipline — diagnose the habit, not your knowledge.</mark>
