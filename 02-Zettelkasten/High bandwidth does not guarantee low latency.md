High bandwidth ≠ low latency.

You can have a "fat pipe" with slow delivery.

Bandwidth = how much data can flow (capacity).
Latency = how long data takes to arrive (delay).

A satellite link can have high bandwidth but 600ms latency.
A fiber link can have both high bandwidth and low latency.
