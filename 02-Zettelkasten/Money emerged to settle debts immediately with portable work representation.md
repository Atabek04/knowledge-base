Money solved perpetual debt's scaling problem by creating immediate settlement.

Instead of "I owe you," money lets one party say: "Here's proof I already worked. This representation of my labor is now yours."

Both parties walk away even. No ongoing debt. No memory required. No trust needed beyond verifying the money is real.

Portability was crucial—the representation of work had to move from hand to hand without special knowledge or relationship.

Money made it possible for strangers to exchange without history, reputation, or ongoing obligation.
