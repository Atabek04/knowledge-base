# Paper money solved the portability problem of metal coins

China pioneered paper money — certificates representing metal stored elsewhere.

Advantages over coins:
- Lighter to carry
- Easier to divide (just write the amount)
- Replaceable if damaged
- Scalable for large transactions

Key tradeoff: requires trust in the issuer.

If the issuer prints too much, inflation destroys value. Metal couldn't be "printed."
