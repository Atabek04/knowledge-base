# Routers have private IP for LAN and public IP for internet

Your home router has two IP addresses:

1. **Private IP** (facing your home network): typically `192.168.1.1`
2. **Public IP** (facing internet): assigned by your ISP

Private IPs are reusable across different networks (NAT handles translation).

Public IPs are globally unique and routable on the internet.
