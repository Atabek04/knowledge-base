### Two modes, two skills

Lichess puzzle modes train *different* abilities — pick the mode for the skill you want:

- <mark style="background: yellow">**Puzzle Storm** — speed and pattern recognition.</mark> Bullet-like volume of repeated basic themes; solve *fast*.
- <mark style="background: yellow">**Puzzle Streak** — deep calculation.</mark> Progressively harder positions that demand real thinking; solve *deep*.
- **Racer** — correct-move count with no time punishment.

---
### Why mix them

Storm grooves the automatic recognition that stops you hanging pieces.
Streak forces the full calculation — <mark style="background: green">seeing the whole solution including the defenses *before* you move.</mark>

<mark style="background: cyan">Storm builds the reflex; Streak builds the depth. You need both.</mark>

This is the practical front-end to [[Re-solving the same chess puzzle set until automatic is the core mechanism of tactics training|tactics-to-automaticity]].

---
### Training discipline

<mark style="background: pink">Don't race for a fast solve on hard puzzles, and don't guess moves</mark> — see the full line first, then play it. Speed is for Storm; accuracy is for Streak.
