British Council's IELTS Ready has two tiers. The free Member tier gives two full mock tests plus short AI-scored mini mocks. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Premium gives roughly 40 full scored practice tests, per-skill self-study courses, AI feedback across all four skills and webinars — free and unlimited, unlocked by booking a British Council IELTS test</mark>, and staying open until seven days after the test date.

This inverts the intuitive order. The reflex is to prepare, then book when ready. The correct order is <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">book first, then diagnose, then prepare inside the library the booking opened</mark>.

The alternative is worse than it used to be. IELTS Progress Check — the only practice test marked by trained IELTS markers, with per-section bands — was discontinued in June 2024. <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">There is no longer any route, free or paid, to human-examiner band scores outside the real exam.</mark> Every band number available in preparation is now either an AI estimate or a raw-score conversion from an answer key.

Since booking is the gate on the best remaining substitute, delaying it costs preparation quality directly.
