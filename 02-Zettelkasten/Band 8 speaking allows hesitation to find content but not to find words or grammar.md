Fluency and Coherence at band 8 is not defined as an absence of pauses. It is defined by what the pauses are *for*.

Band 8: fluent with only very occasional repetition or self-correction, and <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">hesitation may occasionally be used to find words or grammar, but most will be content related</mark>. Band 9 removes even that — hesitation there only prepares the content of the next utterance.

Band 7 is the same phenomenon uncontrolled: some hesitation, repetition or self-correction, <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">often mid-sentence</mark>, indicating problems accessing appropriate language — but not yet affecting coherence. Band 6 is where those problems start costing coherence.

The diagnostic is therefore positional. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">A pause at a clause boundary while you decide what to say reads as thinking; a pause in the middle of a clause reads as a language search.</mark> The same total silence scores differently depending on where it falls.

This also bounds self-correction: it appears in the band 8 line, so correcting yourself is not disqualifying — doing it repeatedly is.
