When you use AI to generate code without reading and understanding every line, you're borrowing confidence from the tool — not building your own.

The result: when the AI is gone, the confidence collapses with it.

This is a root cause of impostor syndrome for developers who vibe-code heavily.
You ship things, but you don't know *how* you shipped them.

---

### The fix

Read every line the AI writes.
If you don't understand a line — ask before moving on.

Use AI as a **reviewer**, not a **author**.
Your understanding must precede the commit.
