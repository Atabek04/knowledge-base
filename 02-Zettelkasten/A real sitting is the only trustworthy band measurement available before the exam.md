Since IELTS Progress Check closed, nothing outside a real sitting produces an examiner-marked band. Listening and Reading can still be self-scored against a Cambridge answer key, so the gap is specific: <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Writing and Speaking have no calibrated measurement available in preparation at all</mark>.

That reframes the exam fee. At roughly $200 in Kazakhstan it is comparable to a prep course, and unlike a course it returns the one number nothing else can supply — which is why teachers routinely advise booking more than one sitting when the budget allows.

Two further arguments for it. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">A marked real script is also the anchor an AI feedback loop needs</mark> to mean anything. And test-day variance is real: reported gains between sittings with targeted work in between cluster around half a band to a full band.

Be careful with the variance argument in the other direction. Posted enquiry-on-results successes are heavily survivorship-biased — nobody posts a remark that changed nothing — and the frequently quoted "most retakes without new input change nothing" figure traces to prep-site copy rather than any study. <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Treat both as folklore and sit again only with a diagnosed fix in hand.</mark>
