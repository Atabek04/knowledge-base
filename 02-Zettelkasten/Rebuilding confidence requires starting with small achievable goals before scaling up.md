Confidence is not rebuilt through ambition — it's rebuilt through evidence.

Small wins create proof that you can do it.
That proof generates the energy (fuel) to attempt the next, harder milestone.

Starting too big when confidence is low leads to failure → reinforced doubt.
Starting small leads to success → momentum → harder goals become reachable.

---

### In practice

**Coding:** Start with a simple REST API. Then add JWT. Then OAuth2.
Don't jump to distributed systems when you're rebuilding trust in yourself.

**Any skill:** The first goal is not mastery — it's proving to yourself that you can move forward.
