### The rule

If your goal is to *improve*, <mark style="background: yellow">play rapid or classical time controls, not bullet or blitz.</mark>

The practical floor recommended by the community is 10+0 or 15+10 rapid; classical is best but impractical for most.

---
### Why

Improvement is [[Deliberate practice builds expert mental representations through focused effort at the edge of current ability|deliberate practice]] — it requires enough time per move to actually calculate, evaluate, and form a plan.

<mark style="background: cyan">Blitz rehearses the speed and pattern-recall you already have; it doesn't build new calculation habits.</mark>

Worse, bullet rewards premoving and *not* checking — <mark style="background: pink">bad habits that bleed into your slower games.</mark> Adults who plateau are very often blitz-dominant.

---
### The honest caveat

This is the community *default*, not a proven optimum.
Coach Dan Heisman argues classical-with-deep-analysis is best and that blitz can beat rapid for raw pattern volume.

The settled part: <mark style="background: green">bullet stalls improvement; play something long enough to run a real thinking process.</mark>
