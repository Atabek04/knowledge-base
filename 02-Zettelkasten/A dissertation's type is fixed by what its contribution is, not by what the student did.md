Students describe their dissertation by activity: "I built an app", "I read forty papers", "I ran a survey". Examiners classify it by contribution, and the two do not line up. The same activity appears in several types; what fixes the type is what the reader is left knowing at the end.

---

### The four types

| Type | You... | Contribution is... |
|---|---|---|
| Empirical | measure something in the world | a **finding** |
| Theoretical | build or extend a model, no new data | a **framework** |
| Systematic review | gather and synthesise every existing study on a question, by a stated search protocol | a **map of what is known** |
| Design science | build an artifact to test an idea, then evaluate it | **knowledge from the evaluation**, never the artifact |

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">A dissertation's type is named by its contribution: a finding, a framework, a map of the literature, or knowledge from evaluating an artifact.</mark>

*Systematic* review is *systematic* because the search is a stated protocol (databases, terms, inclusion criteria) that another person could rerun; a literature review chapter inside another type is not systematic in this sense and does not count as the contribution.

---

### Activity does not decide the type

Building happens in three of the four types: an empirical study needs an instrument, a review needs a search pipeline, design science needs the artifact. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Only in design science is the built thing part of the method being tested; everywhere else it is scaffolding that the contribution does not mention.</mark> That is why "I built an app" says nothing about the type.

The test that fixes the type: what does the reader know at the end that they did not before, and what kind of thing is it. A number, a model, a map, or an evaluation result.

---

### Types as fallbacks

The types are ordered by how much of the world they touch, and that ordering gives a fallback ladder. A [[Design science builds an artifact to test an idea and the knowledge from evaluating it is the contribution|design-science thesis]] whose study never runs still contains a theoretical framework and a protocol; presented as a theoretical dissertation it is weaker but defensible. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">If the empirical part of a design-science thesis slips, retreat one type to theoretical and defend the framework, never to a description of the artifact.</mark> The retreat that fails is the one to no type at all: the bachelor trap, artifact built and evaluation skipped.
