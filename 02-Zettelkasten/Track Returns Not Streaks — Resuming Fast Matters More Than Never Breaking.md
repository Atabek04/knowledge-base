Streak-based tracking creates a fragile system. One missed day feels like failure, and the all-or-nothing mindset causes people to abandon the habit entirely after a single break.

The more resilient metric: how fast do you return after slipping?

## The Research

A study by Phillippa Lally (UCL) found that missing one day had zero statistical impact on long-term habit formation — but missing multiple consecutive days significantly disrupted the pattern.

**One miss is an accident. Two misses in a row is the start of a new habit — the habit of not doing it.**

## Practical Rule

- Miss 1 day → return next day, no self-punishment
- Miss 2 days → drop to minimum viable version immediately
- Miss 3+ days → treat as a fresh start, not a failure

## Identity Framing

Streaks reinforce fragility. Returns reinforce resilience.

"I'm someone who always comes back" is a stronger identity than "I'm someone who never misses."
