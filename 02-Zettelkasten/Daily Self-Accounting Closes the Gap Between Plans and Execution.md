Without a nightly review, the gap between what you planned and what you did remains invisible. You feel busy, but nothing compounds.

A short daily self-accounting session makes the gap visible — and visible gaps get fixed.

## The Minimal Protocol

Three questions before sleeping:
1. What did I actually accomplish toward my goals today?
2. Where did I give in to comfort instead of commitment?
3. What one thing will I do differently tomorrow?

Write it down. One line per question is enough.

## Why Writing Matters

Thinking is cheap — the mind edits memories to feel better. Writing forces honesty. A written record also reveals patterns over days: which tasks you always skip, which times you consistently fail.

## Morning vs Night

Morning planning is fantasy. You're energized and optimistic. Night accounting is reality — it reflects what actually happened when resistance showed up.

**Plan in the morning. Account at night.**
