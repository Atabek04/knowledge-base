### What it is

Chess.com is the polished commercial platform.
Its **Game Review** shows <mark style="background: yellow">accuracy %, move classifications (brilliant / blunder / mistake / inaccuracy / missed win), key moments, and lets you *retry* a flagged mistake.</mark>

---
### Best at

<mark style="background: green">Plain-language "why."</mark>
Lessons, Bots, and Coach explanations tell you the *reason* a move was bad — exactly what [[Lichess provides unlimited free engine analysis and tactics training|Lichess]] leaves to you.

Reach for it when you can't explain your own mistakes.

---
### How to use it well

1. Read the key-moment narration.
2. <mark style="background: cyan">Retry each flagged mistake yourself</mark> — re-finding the better move converts a passive correction into active recall.
3. Drill the suggested puzzles built from your own errors.

---
### Free vs paid

<mark style="background: pink">The depth lives behind the paywall.</mark>
- **Free:** 1 Game Review per day, limited puzzles/lessons/bots, ads.
- **Diamond (~$12–20/mo):** unlimited Game Reviews, Insights, and Coach explanations.

Only pay for Diamond if you'll actually use unlimited reviews; otherwise Lichess covers the engine side for free.
