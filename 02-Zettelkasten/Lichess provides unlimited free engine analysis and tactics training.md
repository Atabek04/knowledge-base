### What it is

Lichess is a free, open-source server where <mark style="background: yellow">every feature is free forever</mark> — analysis, puzzles, studies, stats.
Stockfish runs locally in your browser, so analysis depth and volume are uncapped.

---
### Best at

Unlimited, frictionless <mark style="background: green">analysis and tactics volume with zero paywall.</mark>

- **"Learn from your mistakes"** after any game — cross-checks Stockfish against a masters database and weights each error by *winning-chance change in context* (a +1 swing is only a mistake when the position was still balanced).
- **Analysis board** — multi-line Stockfish + opening explorer over a 2M-game titled database.
- **Puzzles** — 4M+, rated, theme-filtered. [[Puzzle Storm trains fast recognition while Puzzle Streak trains deep calculation|Storm vs Streak]].
- **Studies & Insights** — build repertoire chapters; self-stats on your play.

---
### Limits

<mark style="background: pink">It tells you a move was a mistake but won't explain *why* in plain language</mark> if you can't already find the better move.

For plain-language "why," reach for [[Chess.com Game Review explains your mistakes in plain language|Chess.com Game Review]] — but use the engine only after [[Annotate a chess game yourself before turning on the engine|annotating yourself first]].
