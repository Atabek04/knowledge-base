Every new commitment begins with a surge of enthusiasm. This peak is the worst moment to finalize your targets — because the baseline you'll perform at is during the trough, not the peak.

Goals set at peak enthusiasm get abandoned during the inevitable decline.

## The Cycle

1. **Trigger** — a lecture, a realization, a new year
2. **Enthusiasm peak** — you feel capable of everything
3. **Early execution** — effort feels easy, results seem close
4. **Plateau** — effort is high, results are slow
5. **Decline (فَتْرَة)** — motivation drops, willpower is tested
6. **Abandonment or return** — most quit here; resilient people scale down and continue

## The Rule

Set your targets based on what you'll do on your *worst* day — not your best day. Then protect the minimum.

If you can hit the minimum on bad days, you'll exceed it on good days. If you set the target at peak enthusiasm, you'll miss it on bad days and quit entirely.

## Scaling Down Is Not Failure

Reducing from 20 lines to 3 lines of Quran is not failure — it is intelligent adjustment. The habit survives. From survival, it can grow again.
