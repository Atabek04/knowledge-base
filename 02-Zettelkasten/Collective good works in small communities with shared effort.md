In small tribes, everyone's work benefits everyone. A hunter shares meat with the tribe, knowing others will share the fruits of their labor later.

No formal tracking needed because relationships are direct and long-term. 
You hunt today, someone gathers tomorrow—the community is tight-knit enough to keep implicit score.

Debts aren't settled immediately. 
Instead: "I help you today, you help me tomorrow" without formal record-keeping.

Trust replaces documentation because you see the same people repeatedly and reputations matter instantly.
