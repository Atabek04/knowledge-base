Coherence and Cohesion is the criterion most candidates misread as "use more linking words". The public descriptor for Task 2 shows it is three separate things, and connectives are only one of them.

Band 7 asks for logical organisation with clear progression throughout, a range of cohesive devices used appropriately — explicitly allowing <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">some under- or over-use</mark> — and <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">a central topic in each paragraph</mark>. Band 6 is the same idea failing: cohesion within and between sentences may be faulty or mechanical, and paragraphing exists but is not always logical.

Band 8 does not ask for more devices. It asks for information sequenced logically, all aspects of cohesion managed well, and paragraphing used sufficiently and appropriately.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">So the band-7 ceiling on CC is usually the paragraph, not the connective.</mark> A paragraph with two competing topics fails the band-7 bullet outright; adding "Moreover" to it fixes nothing, and the descriptor already penalises over-use.

The official assessment guide names what carries cohesion instead, and it is the bullet nobody drills: <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">flexible use of reference and substitution</mark> — pronouns, definite articles, *this shift*, *such policies*, varied repetition of key nouns.

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Discourse markers are not the enemy, though, and the popular advice overshoots.</mark> The same official guide lists, as something it assesses, the appropriate use of discourse markers to mark the stages of a response — giving *First of all* and *In conclusion* as its own examples. So the claim that examiners dislike those phrases is wrong. What is penalised is density: band 6 is where cohesion becomes <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">"faulty or mechanical"</mark>, and band 8 is where band 7's under-/over-use clause disappears. A marker at a paragraph transition is doing its job; one opening every sentence is the band-6 marker.

A usable self-test follows. Delete every sentence-initial connector from the essay. If the argument still reads in order, the cohesion was structural and the connectors were decoration. If it collapses, they were doing work the sentences should have been doing.
