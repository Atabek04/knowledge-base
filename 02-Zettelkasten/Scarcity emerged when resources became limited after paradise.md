Before scarcity, no economics was needed—abundance meant infinite resources and no difficult choices.

When humans left abundance, resources became limited and effort was required to survive.

Scarcity forced the first economic problem: how to allocate limited resources.

This created the need for systems to track, exchange, and distribute what was available.
