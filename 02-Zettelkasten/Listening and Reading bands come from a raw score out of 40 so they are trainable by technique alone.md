Listening and Academic Reading are marked out of 40 with no examiner judgement. IELTS publishes the whole-band anchors:

| Band | Listening | Academic Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 35 | 35 |
| 7 | 30 | 30 |
| 6 | 23 | 23 |
| 5 | 16 | 15 |

The half-bands are not officially published. The table reproduced across teacher sites and attributed to the Cambridge practice books gives <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">8.5 at 37, 8.0 at 35-36, 7.5 at 32-34 for Listening and 33-34 for Reading</mark>. Sources differ slightly at the half-band boundaries and agree exactly on the whole-band minimums.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">The official caveat matters more than the disagreement</mark>: the marks required vary slightly from test version to version, which is why Cambridge prints a conversion table inside each test rather than one table for all. Plan for 36, not exactly 35.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The practical consequence is that these two bands are bought in single marks.</mark> Moving Listening from 7.0 to 8.0 is five more correct answers — a countable, diagnosable target of a kind that Writing and Speaking never offer.

Listening uses the same table for Academic and General Training. General Training Reading is scaled harder — band 7 needs 34 and band 8 needs 37.
