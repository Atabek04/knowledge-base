Willpower is a depletable resource. Environment is permanent. Designing your environment to make good actions easy and bad actions hard is more reliable than relying on self-control in the moment.

Kurt Lewin's equation: Behavior = f(Person, Environment). Most people try to change the person. Changing the environment is faster and more durable.

## Friction Reduction (Good Habits)

Make the first step of the desired action require zero effort:
- Mushaf open on the desk before Fajr
- Anki deck open as the browser's default tab
- Exercise mat already on the floor

## Friction Addition (Bad Habits)

Make the undesired action cost more steps:
- Phone in another room during deep work
- Social media apps deleted (not just silenced)
- Unhealthy food not in the house

## The 20-Second Rule

Shawn Achor: if an action requires more than 20 seconds of additional effort, you will avoid it when resistance is high. Reduce setup time for good habits to under 20 seconds.
