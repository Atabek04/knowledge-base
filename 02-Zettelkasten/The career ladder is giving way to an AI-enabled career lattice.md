### Ladder vs lattice

The old model was a **ladder**: one profession, linear rungs, climb for decades.

The emerging model is a **lattice**: <mark style="background: yellow">move sideways between professions as demand shifts, not just upward within one.</mark>

---
### Why AI enables it

Switching fields used to be costly — years of retraining stood in the way.

AI lowers that cost by acting as a personalized tutor and on-the-job assistant, letting workers <mark style="background: cyan">pick up enough of a new domain to be useful quickly.</mark>

The bottleneck shifts from credentials to adaptability.

---
### The strategy it implies

Bet on *learning velocity*, not a single specialization.

<mark style="background: green">Build broad strategic skills and use AI to ramp into whichever field has demand — fluidity becomes the durable advantage.</mark>
