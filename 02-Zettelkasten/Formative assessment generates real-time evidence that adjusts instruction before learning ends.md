### What it is

Formative assessment (Dylan Wiliam & Paul Black, 1998) is a process — not a test type — where evidence of student learning is used by teachers, students, or peers to adjust instruction in real time.

<mark style="background: yellow">Assessment is formative when the evidence changes what happens next.</mark> The same activity can be formative or summative depending on how the evidence is used.

---

### Formative vs summative

- **Summative:** assessment *of* learning — records achievement after learning is complete (grades, exams)
- **Formative:** assessment *for* learning — generates evidence that immediately informs next moves

---

### Wiliam's 5 key strategies

| # | Strategy | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clarify learning intentions and success criteria | Share explicit goals; show exemplars; co-construct rubrics |
| 2 | Engineer effective discussions and tasks | Design questions that make thinking visible |
| 3 | Provide feedback that moves learners forward | Feedback tells students *what to do next*, not just how they did |
| 4 | Activate peers as instructional resources | Structured peer feedback; peer marking |
| 5 | Activate students as owners of their own learning | Self-assessment, metacognitive reflection |

---

### Hinge questions

A hinge question sits at a critical pivot point — the lesson "hinges" here because the teacher's next move depends entirely on the response.

Requirements:
- Takes 1–2 minutes to answer
- Teacher reads the whole-class result in under 30 seconds
- Multiple choice, where each wrong option reveals a specific misconception

*Example:* "Which is larger: 3/4 or 5/8?"
- Students choosing 5/8 reveal they're comparing numerators only
- Students who pick 3/4 correctly may or may not understand *why*

---

### Exit tickets

A 1–3 question check at the end of a lesson. The teacher sorts responses: "got it" / "partially there" / "needs reteaching."

<mark style="background: cyan">Exit tickets inform the *start* of the next lesson, not just the end of this one.</mark>

---

### Online vs offline

**Offline:** Mini-whiteboards for simultaneous hinge question responses; paper exit tickets collected at the door; traffic-light cups (red/amber/green) for ongoing visibility.

**Online:** Google Forms for hinge questions with live response view; exit ticket form auto-graded in under 30 seconds; share anonymized student responses for class discussion.
