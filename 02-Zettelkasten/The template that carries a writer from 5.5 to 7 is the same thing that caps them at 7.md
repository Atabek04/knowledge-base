Advice on IELTS Writing splits by the target band, and the split is usually invisible because both camps are describing something that worked for them.

Below 7, templates work. A fixed four-paragraph skeleton and a stock of connectives guarantee coverage, a stated position and visible organisation — which is most of what bands 5 and 6 are missing.

At 8 the same machinery is the obstacle. A candidate who scored 9 in Writing reported the examiner comment on her own score report: <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">the writing is coherent and cohesive without necessarily any obvious linking words</mark>. Her reading of it — templates lift a band 5 writer to a 7, and at 8-9 you are expected to produce structure without one.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">The mechanism is in the descriptors.</mark> Band 7 already awards coverage, position and appropriate cohesive devices, and explicitly tolerates their over-use. So the template delivers exactly the features you already have, and the bullets that remain — a central topic per paragraph, ideas extended without over-generalising, precise lexis — are the ones a template cannot supply.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Deciding when to stop using the thing that got you here is the actual 7-to-8 problem.</mark>
