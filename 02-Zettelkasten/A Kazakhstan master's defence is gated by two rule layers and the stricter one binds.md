A master's student in Kazakhstan reads the national standard, sees "one publication or one conference talk", and plans for one paper. The university then asks for two, and the student discovers the rule they read was a floor, not the rule. Kazakhstan's defence requirements come in two layers, and the stricter one is the one that applies.

---

### The two layers

**ГОСО** (государственный общеобязательный стандарт образования) is the *state* standard, *obligatory* for every university, and it sets the national minimum. For a master's it requires one publication or one conference presentation before the defence (ГОСО РК 5.04.033-2011, п. 8.10; verify against the current edition, the standard is revised).

The university adds its own rule on top, set by the кафедра or the academic council. A two-article rule is common. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The national standard is a floor the university may raise and never lower, so the university's rule is the one to plan against.</mark>

Only the university can interpret its own rule. What counts as "published", whether conference proceedings count, whether the supervisor must be a co-author, which language: these are кафедра decisions, and the учёный секретарь is who states them in writing. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Never infer the university's reading of a rule from the national text; ask the кафедра in writing months before the deadline, because the answer can cost a whole submission cycle.</mark>

---

### Предзащита and защита

The defence is two events. **Предзащита** is the *pre*-defence, run by the кафедра a month or so before the real one: a rehearsal of the talk, and the point where the file is checked. Antiplagiat runs on the dissertation, the список научных трудов is signed by the учёный секретарь, copies of each publication are attached, and the documents go to the учебный отдел. A file that fails here does not reach the defence.

**Защита** is the *defence* proper, before the state commission. It tests the argument; the paperwork was already tested.

<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Предзащита is where publications are counted, so a paper must satisfy the university's definition of published by that date, not by the defence date a month later.</mark>

---

### The "in press" trap

Journals take three to six months, and many in the КОКСНВО list (the national committee's approved journal list) publish quarterly. Acceptance in March can mean an issue in July, after предзащита in May. Whether an acceptance letter (справка о принятии) suffices is a university question. The safe reading is that published means the issue is out with page numbers or a DOI, and submissions are timed backwards from предзащита with a full review cycle of margin.
