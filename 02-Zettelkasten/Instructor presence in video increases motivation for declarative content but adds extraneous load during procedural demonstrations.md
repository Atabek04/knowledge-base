### The finding

A 2022 meta-analysis (Kizilcec, Bailenson & Gomez, 23 studies) found that instructor-present video — the "talking head" visible alongside content — has a **significant positive effect on motivation and social presence** but a **non-significant overall effect on learning outcomes**.

The critical moderator was knowledge type.

---

### When presence helps vs. hurts

<mark style="background: yellow">**Declarative content**</mark> (vocabulary, facts, definitions, conceptual overviews): instructor presence improves retention and motivation. The face signals warmth and human connection, adding germane load that supports schema formation.

<mark style="background: pink">**Procedural content**</mark> (step-by-step demonstrations, code walkthroughs, diagram analysis): instructor presence adds extraneous load. The learner must split visual attention between the face and the on-screen procedure, occupying working memory without contributing to skill acquisition.

A 2024 eye-tracking study confirmed this: the talking head drew fixations away from content slides when both competed in the frame. Split-attention cost was measurable and consistent.

---

### Production quality matters less than warmth

Guo et al. (2014, 6.9M edX sessions) found informal talking-head segments produced longer average watch times than pure screencast-only. Counterintuitively, informal webcam-level recordings outperformed professional studio productions — because authenticity and warmth signal stronger than production budget.

---

### The design pattern

- **Open with face** — hook, curiosity gap, emotional connection
- **Suppress face during demonstration** — let the content dominate the full frame
- **Return face at close** — summary, encouragement, next step

When the face is visible, **gaze direction matters**: social gaze (looking into camera) builds rapport; guided gaze (looking toward the diagram or code) redirects learner attention to the instructional content. Use social gaze at openings and closings; guided gaze during explanations.
