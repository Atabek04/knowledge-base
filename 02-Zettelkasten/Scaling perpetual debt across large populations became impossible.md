Perpetual debt systems work in tribes of 50-100 people. Everyone remembers who owes whom.

When villages grew to thousands, this collapsed. You couldn't remember:
- Who you helped last year
- What they owed you
- Whether you'd already repaid similar help

Tracking became unreliable. Disputes escalated. Cheating became easier because nobody could verify the full history.

The system hit a hard limit: it didn't scale beyond direct personal relationships.

A new solution was needed to settle debts immediately, without requiring everyone to remember complex obligation chains.
