### The idea

When you solve DSA problems one by one, you memorize solutions.
When you solve by pattern, you build a mental toolkit that transfers to unseen problems.

---
### Common patterns to internalize

- **Sliding window** — optimize subarray/substring problems
- **Two pointers** — reduce O(n²) search to O(n)
- **Fast & slow pointers** — detect cycles
- **Binary search** — eliminate half the search space each step
- **BFS/DFS** — graph and tree traversal
- **Dynamic programming** — overlapping subproblems with optimal substructure
- **Backtracking** — explore decision trees with pruning

---
### How to practice by pattern

Group problems by pattern, not by difficulty or topic label.
Solve 3–5 problems per pattern before moving to the next.
After each problem, ask: *which pattern did this use, and why?*

The goal is to see the pattern in the problem statement, not recognize it after reading the solution.

---
### Why not problem-by-problem

Grinding 300 random LeetCode problems builds familiarity with those 300 problems.
Learning 10 patterns builds the ability to approach any new problem.
