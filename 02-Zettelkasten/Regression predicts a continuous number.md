> **Regression** predicts a <mark style="background: yellow">continuous numerical value</mark> — how much, how many, how far.

---

### What "regression" means in English

The word means **"going back"** or **"returning to a previous state."** If a patient regresses, they got worse after improving.

In the 1880s, **Francis Galton** studied heights of parents and children. He noticed very tall parents had children who were tall but <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">not as extreme</mark> — they "regressed" back toward the average. He called this **"regression toward the mean."**

The mathematical technique he built to study this kept the name. Today we use "regression" for any prediction of a continuous number, even when "returning to the mean" isn't involved.

---

### What makes it regression

The output is a **number on a continuous scale** — it can be any value in a range:
- Price: $150,000 ... $500,000
- Temperature: -10°C ... 45°C
- Percentage: 0% ... 100%

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">If the answer is "how much" or "how many" → it's regression.</mark>
