### The progression

The community-consensus improvement books, <mark style="background: yellow">sequenced by difficulty, not prestige</mark> — work the accessible foundations before the dense classics so the hard book actually lands.

---
### Beginner (~600–1200)

- **Bobby Fischer Teaches Chess** — programmed drilling of one theme (checkmate patterns) via 250+ escalating puzzles. The easiest on-ramp; caps out ~1200.
- **Winning Chess Tactics** (Seirawan) — *understand* each motif (pin, fork, skewer) before drilling. An explainer, too few exercises to build skill alone.
- **Chess Tactics for Champions** (Polgar) — pure high-volume drilling; pair with Seirawan (read to learn the motif, drill to internalize it).

---
### Beginner → Intermediate (~1000–1500)

- **Logical Chess: Move by Move** (Chernev) — 33 games where *every* move is explained; the near-universal "first real strategy book." <mark style="background: green">Understand the purpose of every move, including the quiet ones.</mark>
- **1001 Chess Exercises for Beginners** (Masetti & Messa) — graded puzzle workbook; harder than Polgar, study after it.

---
### Intermediate → Advanced (~1400–2100)

- **The Amateur's Mind** (Silman) — diagnose flawed amateur thinking; gentler prequel to Reassess.
- **How to Reassess Your Chess** (Silman) — the full imbalance system: [[Silman's imbalances method chooses a plan from the position's static features|find the imbalances, then plan]].
- **Silman's Complete Endgame Course** / **100 Endgames You Must Know** (de la Villa) — [[Learn the endgames for your rating level not exhaustive endgame theory|endgames by level / the recurring 100]].
- **The Woodpecker Method** (Smith & Tikkanen) — tactics as a [[Re-solving the same chess puzzle set until automatic is the core mechanism of tactics training|re-solve-to-automaticity]] system.

---
### Classic, read later

- **My System** (Nimzowitsch) — prophylaxis, overprotection, blockade, outposts. Revered but dense and idiosyncratic; <mark style="background: pink">read Silman first, then My System.</mark>

<mark style="background: cyan">Rating ranges are community convention, not official — match the book to your weakest phase.</mark>
