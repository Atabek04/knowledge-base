> A **tangent line** touches the curve at exactly one point.
> Its slope = the [[Derivative measures how fast something is changing at a specific point|derivative]] at that point.

---

### Example

At x = 1 on $y = x^2$:
- Point: (1, 1)
- Derivative: $f'(1) = 2(1) = 2$
- Tangent line: $y = 2x - 1$

```functionplot
---
title: Tangent Line at x = 1
xLabel: x
yLabel: y
bounds: [-1, 3, -1, 5]
grid: true
---
y = x^2
y = 2*x - 1
```

**What this shows:**
- Tangent line (red) touches curve at x = 1
- Its slope (2) = derivative at that point
- Line shows **instantaneous rate of change**

---

### Three ways to say the same thing

- Derivative at x = 1 is 2
- [[Slope measures how much y changes when x changes|Slope]] of tangent line is 2
- Rate of change at x = 1 is 2
