### What gets cheap

Unlimited robotic labor can drive the price of most goods and services toward their material cost.

When labor is free, <mark style="background: yellow">anything reproducible becomes near-free.</mark>

---
### What stays expensive

Not everything is reproducible.

**Land cannot be manufactured.** Neither can raw materials in fixed supply, nor energy at the margin.

<mark style="background: cyan">Scarcity migrates from labor to the things robots cannot print — and value migrates with it.</mark>

---
### The implication

In a hyperabundant economy, owning *scarce* assets matters far more than earning income.

This is why the post-labor inequality story centers on land, energy, and compute rather than salaries.
