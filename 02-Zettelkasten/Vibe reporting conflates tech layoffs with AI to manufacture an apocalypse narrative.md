### What "vibe reporting" is

Cal Newport's term for journalism that builds a **predetermined narrative through omission and suggestion** rather than explicit, checkable claims.

<mark style="background: yellow">It implies "AI is taking jobs" without ever directly asserting it — so it can't be fact-checked.</mark>

---
### The three moves

- **Omits relevant facts** — leaves out the publicly stated reason for a layoff.
- **Suggestive placement** — interleaves AI quotes next to layoff news to imply causation.
- **Appeals to biased authorities** — quotes AI CEOs, who have a stake in sounding world-altering.

A Deutsche Bank analyst mocked one such piece for a <mark style="background: green">"vibes-to-substance ratio" that was "undeniably high."</mark>

---
### The concrete example

Coverage framing **Amazon's** layoffs as AI-driven omits that those cuts began in **2022–2023, before the generative-AI boom**, and targeted **management bureaucracy** from pandemic over-hiring.

---
### Why it's harmful

<mark style="background: pink">Vibe reporting creates "a nihilistic sense of inevitable disruption" that discourages action.</mark>

Newport's counter: *"Nothing about these tools is inevitable, and their impact is far from preordained."*
