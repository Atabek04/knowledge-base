### The claim

At amateur level, <mark style="background: yellow">the overwhelming majority of decisive games are won or lost on a tactic — a missed fork, a hanging piece, a one-move blunder — not on strategy, plans, or opening preparation.</mark>

Coaches estimate ~95% of sub-2000 games turn on a tactical error.

---
### Why this sets your priorities

If tactics decide the games, <mark style="background: cyan">tactics is the dominant lever for rating gain</mark> below ~1800.

Strategy and positional understanding both get *easier* once tactical vision sharpens, because you stop losing the pieces your plans depend on.

This is the foundation of the whole sub-1800 study order: [[Deep opening memorization is wasted below 1800 ELO so learn principles not lines|tactics before openings]].

---
### What "do tactics" means

Solve puzzles daily.
Build pattern recognition until common motifs are *recognized*, not calculated from scratch — see [[Re-solving the same chess puzzle set until automatic is the core mechanism of tactics training]].

<mark style="background: pink">"I lost because of my opening" is almost always false below 1600 — you lost to a tactic several moves later.</mark>
