A bottleneck is the link with the lowest bandwidth in a network path.

It limits the throughput of the entire connection.

If data passes through links of 1 Gbps → 100 Mbps → 1 Gbps, the effective throughput caps at 100 Mbps.

Identifying and upgrading bottlenecks is key to improving network performance.
