One lesson a day, every day, compounds faster than a 5-hour session once a week.

The mechanism: each small session reduces friction for the next one.
Irregular sessions feel like starting over each time — the warm-up cost never disappears.

---

### Why it works

- Keeps the topic in working memory between sessions
- Builds a streak identity: "I'm someone who does this daily"
- Lowers the activation energy — "just one lesson" is easy to start

---

### In practice

- **ML / DevOps:** One lesson per day. Take notes. Create a flashcard. Review.
- **Language:** 50 minutes daily. Start the timer regardless of energy or motivation.
- **Memorization:** 5 lines per day. Prove you can do it. Then increase the size.

The rule: start the timer no matter what.
Motivation follows action — not the other way around.
