Bandwidth is the maximum rate of data transfer over a network connection.

Measured in bits per second (bps, Mbps, Gbps).

Analogy: bandwidth is the pipe diameter, not the water flow speed.

A wider pipe can carry more water per second, but doesn't make water move faster.
