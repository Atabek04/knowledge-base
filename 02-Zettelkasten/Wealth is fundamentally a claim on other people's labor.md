### The core idea

Wealth is not the paper or the number in an account.

Wealth is a <mark style="background: yellow">claim on other people's time and effort</mark> — the right to direct labor toward what you want.

Almost everything you consume — a chip, a meal, a surgery — is the end of a long chain of human work. Money is the token that lets you pull on that chain.

---
### Why this framing matters

If wealth is stored labor, then anything that changes the cost of labor changes what wealth *is*.

This is the hinge for every argument about AI and money: when machines can supply labor, the value of a claim on human labor is no longer obvious.

<mark style="background: cyan">Hold this definition first — the AI-economy predictions are all consequences of it.</mark>
