Lexical Resource is scored on two independent axes — range and precision — and the band 8 descriptor deliberately forgives one of them.

Band 7 asks for a sufficient range allowing some flexibility and precision, less common items used with some awareness of style and collocation, and <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">occasional errors in word choice, spelling or word formation</mark>. Band 8 asks for a wide range used fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings, uncommon items used skilfully — while explicitly permitting <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation</mark> — with only rare spelling errors.

So the wrong word occasionally does not block band 8. A narrow range does.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">This kills the memorised-synonym strategy from the other direction too.</mark> "Skilfully uses uncommon lexical items" is a judgement about fit, not rarity: a rare word inserted where a common one belongs reads as an inaccuracy in word choice, which at band 7 is already a listed weakness, and it does not buy range because range is about how many different meanings you can express, not how obscure the words are.

The wording is identical for Task 1 and Task 2.
