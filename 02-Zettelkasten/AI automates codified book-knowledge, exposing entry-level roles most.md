### Why the young get hit first

It seems backward — shouldn't experience be the thing AI replaces?

The opposite is true. Entry-level work is mostly <mark style="background: yellow">codified knowledge: the "book-learning" from formal education and routine implementation tasks.</mark>

That is exactly what LLMs absorbed from their training data and reproduce cheaply.

---
### What AI can't yet copy

Senior workers trade in <mark style="background: cyan">tacit knowledge</mark> — judgment, context, relationships, knowing which rule to break — that isn't written down anywhere to be learned from.

So AI erodes the bottom rung of the ladder while leaving the higher rungs intact.

---
### The trap it creates

If juniors can't get hired to do implementation, they never accumulate the tacit experience that makes seniors valuable.

<mark style="background: pink">Automating the entry rung may starve the pipeline that produces senior talent.</mark>
