
<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Scarcity is the core problem</mark>: resources are finite, but ways to use them are endless.

You have 24 hours (limited). You can work, sleep, learn, eat, relax (unlimited uses).

You have $100 (limited). You can buy food, entertainment, save, invest, donate (unlimited uses).

Because you can't do everything, you must **choose**. <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Economics studies how to make those choices wisely</mark>.

This is why money, trade, and markets exist—they help coordinate these choices across millions of people.
