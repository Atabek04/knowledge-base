### Two ways AI meets a job

The same tool can do opposite things to employment depending on *how* it's used.

- **Automate** — AI does the task instead of the human → the role shrinks.
- **Augment** — AI helps the human do the task faster → the role often grows.

<mark style="background: yellow">Job losses concentrate where AI substitutes; they stay muted where AI complements.</mark>

---
### Why the distinction drives measurement

Anthropic's "observed exposure" measure weights *automated* uses fully and *augmenting* uses at half — because real displacement comes from substitution, not assistance.

A high theoretical capability doesn't displace anyone until it's actually used to replace, not assist.

---
### The practical takeaway

How you adopt AI decides whether it threatens your role.

<mark style="background: green">Use AI to extend what only you can do (strategy, judgment) and you augment yourself; hand it the whole task and you automate yourself out.</mark>
