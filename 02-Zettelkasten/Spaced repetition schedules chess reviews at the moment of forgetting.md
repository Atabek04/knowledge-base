### What it is

Spaced repetition surfaces each item for review <mark style="background: yellow">just as you're about to forget it</mark>, reviewing hard lines more often than mastered ones.

Chessable's **MoveTrainer** applies this to chess: read commentary on a move → play it → repeat the whole line as a sequence, on an auto-scheduled cadence.

---
### Best for

<mark style="background: green">Memorizing openings</mark> — and tactics/endgame patterns — for long-term retention.
Its real value is automating what's too tedious to track by hand: dates, intervals, which lines you got wrong. *"You just need to show up."*

This is the same engine as [[Spaced repetition schedules vocabulary review at expanding intervals matched to the forgetting curve|vocabulary SRS]], applied to chess lines.

---
### Honest caveat on the evidence

<mark style="background: pink">The general spaced-repetition science is strong; the chess-platform-specific proof is testimonial, not a controlled study.</mark>

Don't conflate "SR works in general" with "this app is proven to raise rating."
Free open-source alternative for drilling openings: Chessdriller.

---
### Where it fits the order

SR memorizes lines — so it pays off *after* tactics are solid and you've chosen [[A small chess opening repertoire kept for a year beats collecting many openings|a small repertoire to deepen]].
