The band 7 and band 8 Task Achievement bullets differ in exactly two places, and both are about data rather than language.

Band 7 "clearly presents and highlights key features" carries the qualifier <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">but could be more fully extended</mark>. Band 8 drops that qualifier and inserts a third verb: presents, highlights and <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">illustrates</mark> key features clearly and appropriately. Band 7's "covers the requirements" also becomes "covers **all** requirements sufficiently".

Illustrating means every feature you named in the overview is then supported with figures taken from the chart. So a band-8 Task 1 script cannot be figure-free — and band 5 confirms the floor from below, warning that there may be no data to support the description.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">The descriptors punish both extremes.</mark> Band 5 is also where "recounts detail mechanically" and "a tendency to focus on details" sit, and band 6 is where details "may be irrelevant, inappropriate or inaccurate". Listing every number is a band 5-6 behaviour, not a safe one.

The binding constraint at 8 is therefore which features you pick and which figures you attach to them — though this governs one criterion of four, since Lexical Resource and Grammar carry half the Task 1 mark between them.
