In small communities, debts were perpetual—never fully settled, always outstanding.

You don't repay a debt with something equivalent and walk away. Instead, obligations flow continuously: "I owe you" becomes "you owe me" becomes "I owe you again."

This works because:
- Everyone knows everyone
- Reputation for fairness matters long-term
- Violation damages future cooperation
- Relationships span decades

The system assumes trust and face-to-face accountability.
