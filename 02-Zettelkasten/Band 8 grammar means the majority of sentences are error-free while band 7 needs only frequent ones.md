Grammatical Range and Accuracy is the one criterion the public descriptor states as something close to a countable quantity, which makes it the easiest to self-audit.

Band 7 wants a variety of complex structures, <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">frequent error-free sentences</mark>, and good control with a few errors. Band 8 wants a wide range of structures, <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">the majority of sentences error-free</mark>, and only very occasional errors or inappropriacies.

The gap between "frequent" and "the majority" is the whole 7 → 8 move on this criterion, and it is a subtraction problem, not an addition one: <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">band 8 is reached by removing recurring errors, not by adding harder structures.</mark>

Concrete audit: count sentences in your essay, count sentences containing any error, and the error-carrying sentences must fall below half. For a Turkic-L1 writer the count is usually dominated by one repeating category — articles — which is a single fixable habit rather than a general weakness.

The wording is identical for Task 1 and Task 2.
