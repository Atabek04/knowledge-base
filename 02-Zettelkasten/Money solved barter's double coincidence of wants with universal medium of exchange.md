In pure barter, both parties need what the other has at the same time.

You have apples and want wheat. But the wheat farmer wants fish, not apples. The trade fails because there's no "double coincidence of wants."

Money solves this: you trade apples for money (everyone wants money), then trade money for wheat with anyone.

The key shift is that money doesn't need to match individual needs—it just needs to be something everyone in the community accepts as valuable.

**Example**:
- With barter: You're stuck with apples unless you find someone who both has wheat AND wants apples specifically
- With money (gold coins): You trade apples for coins with anyone, then find a wheat seller. Solved.
