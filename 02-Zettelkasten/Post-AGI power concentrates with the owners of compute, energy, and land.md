### The new inequality

If goods get cheap but wages collapse, equality does *not* follow.

Power flows to whoever owns the things that stay scarce: <mark style="background: yellow">the AI models, the compute, the energy, and the land they sit on.</mark>

Workers lose their one bargaining chip — labor — while owners gain near-total control over access to essentials.

---
### Why the ultra-wealthy act the way they do

This thesis reads today's billionaire behavior as positioning for that world:

- Heavy bets on **energy and space** (solar, land, raw materials).
- Massive capital raised to **own frontier AI infrastructure**.

The goal is to <mark style="background: cyan">"secure a spot" on the owning side of the AGI divide</mark> before the divide hardens.

---
### Why it's not inevitable

Concentration is the *default*, not the destiny.
Robust antitrust, anti-corruption law, and redistribution are the proposed counterweights.

<mark style="background: pink">Without policy, cheap goods coexist with extreme power imbalance — abundance is not the same as fairness.</mark>
