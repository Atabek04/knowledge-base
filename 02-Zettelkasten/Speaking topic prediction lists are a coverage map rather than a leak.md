Quarterly "IELTS Speaking forecast" lists circulate constantly and are sold as leaked questions. They are not leaks — IELTS publishes no speaking topics, and anything marketed as guaranteed is guesswork or a scam.

The underlying signal is nonetheless real. Test takers report their cue cards afterwards, several trackers aggregate them, and <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Part 2 cards genuinely do recur within a test cycle</mark>.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The correct use is as a coverage map</mark>: run down the themes and check that none is completely unrehearsed. Work, media, education, environment, technology, health, city life.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">The incorrect use is one memorised script per predicted card</mark>, which manufactures the strong-Part-2 profile examiners are trained to catch.

A list is also perishable. For a February sitting, anything published before the preceding December describes a cycle that has already turned over.
