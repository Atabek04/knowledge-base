```
AI (biggest box)
└─ ML (inside AI)
   └─ DL (inside ML)
      └─ GenAI (uses DL)
```
## AI

**Artificial Intelligence** - system that **imitates human intelligence**
- Reasoning
- Recognizing images
- Understanding speech
## ML

**Machine Learning** - subset of AI, where system **learn patterns from data**
	instead of being hard-coded with rules (if-else)

## DL

**Deep Learning** - subset of ML that uses **neural networks with many layers** to learn complex patterns.

## GenAI

**Generative AI** - AI systems (usually DL-based) that **create new content**

They don't just classify or predict.
**They generate**.

```
GenAI (generates content)
├─ LLMs (generate text) ← ChatGPT, GPT-4
├─ Image generators (DALL-E, Midjourney)
├─ Music generators
└─ Video generators
```
