## To create quotes: start line with `>`

> This is one line quote

## For multi-line quotes, just keep starting new lines w/ `>`

> Now this is example of multi-line
> next line
> next line

## For nested quotes, increament the `>` to `>>` or `>>>` and etc.

> This is 1st level
>> Second level
>>> Third level
>> Removed one `>` now I have only `>>`
> Now in this level, I have only `>`

### Not following sequence: `>>>` right after `>`

> This level 
>>> here I have `>>>`
>>> I dont' is is looking good or not, but you can do it.
