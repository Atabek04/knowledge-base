
debasement
the action of reducing the quality or value of something
Currency debasement means that the more dollars there are in circulation, the less each dollar is worth.

deplete
to reduce something in size or amount, especially supplies of energy, money, etc.
The illness depletes the body of important vitamins.

Perpetual debt
debt that never ends

Scarcity
a situation in which something is not easy to find or get
the scarcity of skilled workers

Ransom
demanding payment/concessions in exchange for returning something (or someone) you're holding.
"The hacker held the company's data as ransom, demanding $1 million to restore access."

forgery
an illegal copy of a document, painting, etc. 
the crime of making such illegal copies
These banknotes are forgeries.
