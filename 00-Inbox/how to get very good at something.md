
You may lose confident because of using too much vibe-coding.

To gain confidence again, you simply need to build projects by yourself.
To have that energy and feeling when building something great.

We can have two approaches:
1. Theoretical knowledges should be written as flashcards and reviewed. When you answer to flashcards with precision, you gain some confidence.
2. When you build something powerful, you'll gain confidence.



---

### Start from something is easy

Here the goal is to gain confidence.
By achieving small goals, you get fuel to achieve other next milestones.

That's why in the beginning you should start with small, but achievable goals!

#### Coding Projects

For example just start by implementing simple REST application.

After you done, start adding different type of security: JWT, OAuth2 etc.

As well as do different projects in Python.
Games like Hungman, Sudoku.
Learn PyGame and implement some games.
Learn WebSocket and implement multiplayer games.

#### Take just one lesson every day for ML, DevOps etc.

Every day learn a new lesson and make sure that you understood the topic.
Take notes. Create flashcard. Review.

If it's practical knowledge, start to practice with simple tasks.
Gain confidence and move on to harder problems.

#### Same with Islamic Studies

Every day memorize just 5 lines.
Gain confidence. Show that you can do it.
Then increase the size.
Every day 50 min. of Arabic class.
Start the timer, no matter of what. Even if you're tired and don't have motivation.
