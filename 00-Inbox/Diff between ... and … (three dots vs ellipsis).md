## I know they look the same

### But they aren't

Because second ellipsis isn't considered as a typo.
But if you type ..., then any editor will tell you "what's that ?! Use ellipsis!"
Even obsidian right now, telling that. Just look at the three dots in second line.

Here's the difference:
- **`...`** (three dots) = Three separate period characters
- **`…`** (ellipsis) = Single Unicode character designed for proper typography

>The ellipsis looks better, 
> doesn't break awkwardly across lines, 
> It is the correct typographical standard.
### How to type it ?

### Windows:

```
Alt + 0133 (on numeric keypad)
```
