skill applies here too: point at a specific unit the user owns, never at "a grammar book".

You can read a unit directly when the user asks what one covers:
`pdftotext -f <page> -l <page> -layout "<path>" -`. Murphy's unit *n* is roughly page
`2n + 8` in the PDF; check rather than assume.

raw-score conversion tables. *Cambridge Grammar for IELTS* is **not** owned and is not

## Murphy — the schedule the plan runs

117 of the 145 units, **7 a week**, October to January. Cover to cover does not fit: four a
week reaches unit 70 by February.

| Month | Units | What lands |
|---|---|---|
| **Oct** | 6–37 | Present perfect & continuous, perfect vs past, past perfect, future, modals |
| **Nov** | 38–48, 53–71 | Conditionals & *wish*, passive incl. *it is said that…*, reported speech, *-ing and to…*, countability |
| **Dec** | 72–76, 79–97, 98–104 | **Articles**, plurals, determiners, **relative clauses**, *-ing/-ed* clauses, adjectives & adverbs |
| **Jan** | 105–120, 129–136 | Comparatives, word order, *although/unless/as long as*, noun/adj/verb + preposition |
| **Jan, last** | Study Guide p. 326 | Maps wrong answers to unit numbers — redo only what it flags |
| **Feb** | — | Nothing new |

**Skipped, and why** — 1–5 already the working inventory · 49–52 questions and tags,
Speaking-only · 77–78 names with/without *the* · 121–128 place and time prepositions
(129–136 are kept: those are the ones that cost marks) · 137–145 phrasal verbs, wrong
register for Task 2.

Units worth naming by number in an audit, because they answer the commonest findings:

- **68** *-ing clauses* — the participle clause that keeps showing as absent in the range inventory
- **72–76** the article block — the dominant error category for this writer
- **92–96** relative clauses · **97** *-ing* and *-ed* clauses
- **42–46** the passive, including reporting passives
- **38–41** conditionals and *wish*
- **7–16** perfect aspect — the structure Task 1 trend description needs

If a month runs short, units are **dropped**, not the rate halved. Say so when the user
proposes catching up at three a week.

53 is the most reusable unit in the book for Task 2. 46 and 48 are what Task 1 is made of.
Units 1–5 are method, read once and never carded.

**English Vocabulary in Use Advanced stays shut this cycle** — C1/C2 lexis on a three-tense
grammar base produces the rare-word-in-the-wrong-slot error that Lexical Resource scores as
an inaccuracy while buying no range. Only if January runs ahead: units 80 (Academic writing:
text structure), 81 (Writing: style and format), 70 (Modality), 71 (Number: statistics).

## How to use this in each mode

**audit** — the two study targets that close a Writing audit must be unit numbers from the
tables above, not topic names. "Articles: generic reference and zero article with
uncountables — Murphy 72–76, and 69–71 for the countability half" is usable; "work on
articles" is not.

**drill** — if the fault is a grammar structure, the drill is that Murphy unit's exercises
plus one essay paragraph using the structure. Exercises alone do not transfer.

**Anything the user has not reached yet** — say so and give the unit number anyway, marked
as ahead of schedule. Knowing the fix exists at unit 68 is worth more than a vague promise
that participle clauses come later.
