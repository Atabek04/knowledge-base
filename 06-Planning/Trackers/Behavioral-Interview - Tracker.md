## 1. Foundations

- [ ] Learn STAR-L structure (Situation/Task/Action/Result/Learning)
- [ ] Map the 8 target competencies (ownership, conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, technical decision, communication, growth)
- [ ] Read the "select stories, don't memorize" principle and internalize it

## 2. Story Bank — Draft (atomic note per story)

- [ ] Story 1: Biggest ownership win (end-to-end project)

- [ ] Story 4: Influence without authority
- [ ] Story 5: Ambiguity navigated (unclear requirements)
- [ ] Story 6: Hard technical tradeoff defended
- [ ] Story 7: Growth / sought feedback

## 3. Story Bank — Competency Coverage Check

- [ ] Tag each story with the competencies it covers
- [ ] Verify all 8 competencies are covered by at least one story
- [ ] Confirm each story has a quantified Result
- [ ] Confirm each story uses "I" (own contribution clear)

## 4. Rehearsal (out loud, recorded)

- [ ] Rehearse all 7 stories to 2-3 min each

- [ ] Mock behavioral session 1
- [ ] Mock behavioral session 2

## 5. Company Research Routine

- [ ] Build a reusable per-company research checklist
- [ ] Practice mapping 7 stories to one target company's values
- [ ] Prepare a bank of 3-4 questions to ask interviewers
