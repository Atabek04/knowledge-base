Which test, on which day, from which book. Tick as you go; the tag list from each one goes in [[IELTS-Log]].

Platform: **[IELTS Mock Lab](https://ieltsmocklab.com)** — Cambridge 11–20 on a computer-delivered interface, no signup. `/ielts-listening` and `/ielts-reading`, each with a **Full Tests** tab and a **Single Passage** tab.

### The inventory, so nothing gets burned early

| | Books available | Full tests |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 11–20 (Book 10 has none) | **40** |
| Reading | 11–20, plus one test from Book 10 | **41** |

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Books 19 and 20 are reserved for the January mocks and are not opened before then.</mark> They are the closest to the current exam, and their only irreplaceable use is as a clean, never-seen mock. Everything before January comes from 11–18.

Grading: **IELTSFreeTests** auto-grades with the official raw→band conversion — use it to save the marking time. Mock Lab's own band estimate is a rough banding, not a conversion table; trust the raw score out of 40 and convert from the Cambridge volume.

## October — technique. Single sections and passages, not full tests

Full tests repeat the same four question types you already know you fail. Sections isolate them.

| Week (Mon) | Mon · L section | Wed · L section | Fri · L section | Tue · R passage | Thu · R passage | Sun · full test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5 Oct** | C11 T1 P1 | C11 T1 P2 | C11 T1 P3 | C11 T1 p1 | C11 T1 p2 | C12 T1 Listening |
| **12 Oct** | C11 T2 P1 | C11 T2 P2 | C11 T2 P3 | C11 T2 p1 | C11 T2 p2 | C12 T1 Reading |
| **19 Oct** | C11 T3 P1 | C11 T3 P2 | C11 T3 P3 | C11 T3 p1 | C11 T3 p2 | C12 T2 Listening |
| **26 Oct** | C11 T4 P1 | C11 T4 P2 | C11 T4 P3 | C11 T4 p1 | C11 T4 p2 | C12 T2 Reading |

Every section is followed by tagging each wrong answer by category — *word form · spelling · word-limit breach · distractor followed · lost place* for Listening, by question type for Reading. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">A section sat without its tag list is a measurement, not practice.</mark>

Part 4 of Listening and passage 3 of Reading are deliberately absent from the weekday rota — they are the hardest and belong in a full test where fatigue is part of the exercise.

## November — same shape, harder material, plus the mid-point re-test

| Week (Mon) | L sections (Mon/Wed/Fri) | R passages (Tue/Thu) | Sun · full test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2 Nov** | C13 T1 P1–3 | C13 T1 p1–2 | C14 T1 Listening |
| **9 Nov** | C13 T2 P1–3 | C13 T2 p1–2 | C14 T1 Reading |
| **16 Nov** | C13 T3 P1–3 | C13 T3 p1–2 | C14 T2 Listening |
| **23 Nov** | C13 T4 P1–3 | C13 T4 p1–2 | C14 T2 Reading |
| **30 Nov** | — re-test week — | | **C15 T1 Listening + Reading, back to back, timed** |

The 30 November sitting is the checkpoint. Convert with the Cambridge volume's own table, not an online one. Both at 8.0+ → the route holds. Either under 7.5 → that skill takes the January slack.

## December — production. Full tests, timed, on the CD interface

The weekday hour cannot hold a 60-minute reading test plus review, so one full test is split across two days and the other is sat whole on Sunday.

| Week (Mon) | Split across Tue + Thu | Sun · whole, timed |
|---|---|---|
| **7 Dec** | C15 T2 Reading | C15 T2 Listening |
| **14 Dec** | C16 T1 Reading | C16 T1 Listening |
| **21 Dec** | C16 T2 Reading | C16 T2 Listening |
| **28 Dec** | C17 T1 Reading | C17 T1 Listening |

Listening sections stay on Mon/Wed/Fri from C15–C17 wherever the week has room. From here on **every test is sat on the Mock Lab interface**, never on paper — the interface should be boring by February.

---

## January — rehearsal. Four full four-section mocks, one per weekend

Each mock takes Saturday; its post-mortem takes Sunday. The essay loop moves to the weekdays those weeks.

| Weekend | Mock | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sat 9 Jan** | **Cambridge 17 Test 2** — L + R + W + S | First full-length rehearsal. Expect the score to disappoint; stamina is the variable being tested |
| **Sat 16 Jan** | **Cambridge 18 Test 1** | Compare the shape of the errors, not the band |
| **Sat 23 Jan** | **Cambridge 19 Test 1** — *reserved book* | The honest one. Section bands against the route |
| **Sat 30 Jan** | **Cambridge 20 Test 1** — *reserved book* | Closest to the current exam. Last full mock |

Weekdays that month: C18 T2–T4 as single full tests, and whatever the post-mortems flag.

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">C19 T2–T4 and C20 T2–T4 stay unopened.</mark> If February becomes a calibration sitting and a retake follows, they are the only clean material left.

---

## February — taper

No new tests. One light section a day from a book already sat, purely to keep the ear in. Re-read the descriptor pages and your own error log.

## The other platforms, and what each is for

| Site | Use it for | Not for |
|---|---|---|
| **IELTS Mock Lab** | Every timed test. CD interface, Cambridge 11–20, no signup | — |
| **IELTSFreeTests** | Auto-grading L+R with the official conversion — saves ~15 min per test | Sitting the test; the interface is not the exam's |
| **OneIELTS**, **IELTS Test Simulation** | Backup when Mock Lab is down | Extra volume — same Cambridge content, already counted |
| **Engnovate** | Reading solutions during review, when a Not Given answer makes no sense | Sitting tests |

Same Cambridge material behind most of them. More sites do not mean more practice; more hours do.
