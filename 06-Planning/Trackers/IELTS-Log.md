## Pre-mock checklist

Read before the next mock. Revised 05 Sep.

1. **Read each question once.** Don't understand it? Mark it, move on — the audio explains it. Re-reading is what broke the last mock.
2. **Sit it rested**, never after another study session.
3. **Decide singular/plural from the stem's grammar** before the audio. Never `car(s)`.
4. **If an answer only makes sense logically, that's a warning.** Write what was said.
5. **Part 4 gets the attention budget**, not the leftovers.
6. **Screenshot every review screen before closing the tab.**
