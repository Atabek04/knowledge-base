Every subject below is a **map of content**: a reading order over atomic notes, each note one idea with a title that states it. Open a map, follow its sections top to bottom, and use the sidebar, graph and backlinks to see what each idea connects to.

### How the notes are written

- **One idea per note.** The title is the claim; the body is why it holds.
- **Highlights carry meaning.** Yellow is a definition, cyan a mechanism, blue a rule, red a warning.
- **Links are the structure.** A note never re-teaches a neighbour's concept; it links to it.
