English Card
Someone people enjoy spending time with → *you are good ______*
Back: **good company**.
- No article: *she's good company*, never *a good company* — that is a business
- Speaking Part 1/2 when describing a friend

English Card
An occasional tendency to act selfishly → a selfish ______
Back: **a selfish streak** — one bad thread running through an otherwise fine character.
- *streak* = a strip of a different colour; the metaphor is a stripe in the personality
- Same pattern: **a stubborn streak**, **a ruthless streak**

English Card

Back: **start a family** (NOT *begin*) · **have children** (NOT *get*) · **expect a baby** (NOT *wait for*) · **have a baby** (NOT *get*).

Which verbs build a friendship, and which one is wrong?
Back: **strike up a friendship** (start one, often by chance) · **form / develop a friendship** — never *make a friendship*.
