START
English Card
To make sure people actually obey a law → ______ the law
Back: **uphold the law** or **enforce the law** — two that work.
- The job of the police, not of the legislature
- Contrast the whole chain: parliament **passes** a law, citizens **obey/observe** it, police **enforce** it

For a parliament to create a new law → ______ a law

START
English Card
A punishment that is very severe → a ______ penalty / a ______ fine
Back: **a harsh penalty** or **a harsh sentence**; money is different — **a heavy fine**.
- You **pay** or **face** a heavy fine; a criminal may **face the death penalty**
- Never *a strong fine* or *a hard penalty* — the adjective changes with the noun

Back: **the crime rate** — it **comes down** or **falls**.
