To pass a period of time somewhere → ______ time
Back: **spend time** — *I spent some time in South America.*
- Never *passed time* in this sense; *reading passes the time* only works with the activity as subject
- *spend*, *waste* and *save* all take **time** and **money** alike

Which verbs go with *a walk*, *a run* and *a stroll*?
Back: **go for a walk / a run / a stroll** — and *take a stroll* also works, though *go for* is commoner in speech.
- Travelling without a vehicle is **on foot**, not *by foot*
- A fast one is **a brisk walk**; a slow relaxed one is **a leisurely stroll**
