START
English Card
To start living in a new flat or house → ______ a flat
Back: **move into a flat** — and the opposite, **move out of** a flat.
- Not *move to a flat* — *move to* takes a city or country, *move into* takes the dwelling
- Russian *переехать в квартиру* predicts the missing *in*
