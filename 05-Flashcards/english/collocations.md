Research that opens a new field → ______ research
Back: **groundbreaking research**
- Also **pioneering** · **original** — three that work
- The whole phrase is what carries the praise; *very new research* says nothing

Which verbs go with *research*, and which one is the classic error?
Back: **do research** or **carry out research** — never *make research*.
- *conduct research* is the most formal of the three and fits Task 2
- Listed under the unit's *Common mistakes*, and Russian *делать* is why *make* feels right

To offer a theory for others to consider → ______ a theory
Back: **put forward a theory**, or **propose a theory**.
- Never *give a theory* — flagged in the book as a common mistake
- The counter-move has its own collocation: *Simon Hart **challenges** the theory*

A paper that genuinely advances its field → makes a ______ contribution
Back: **a significant contribution to the field**
- Also **major** · **important** — three that work
- *significant* does double duty in academic prose: it also collocates with **trends**, as in *the statistics do not show any significant trends*

START
English Card
Proof so complete that it cannot be argued against → ______ proof
Back: **irrefutable proof** — literally proof that cannot be refuted.
- Weaker sibling: *he does not **offer** irrefutable proof* — proof is **offered**, not *given*
- Related: **supporting evidence**, which you *offer*; and evidence you **gather** before you *carry out a detailed study*

START
English Card
How do you mark an opinion as *strong* in academic writing? *(three collocations)*
Back: **the key factor** · **a perfect example** · **a clear illustration**
- *the **key factor** in the birth of the industrial revolution*
- *a **perfect example** of how political leaders make misjudgements*
- *a **clear illustration** of the importance of a strong monetary policy*

START
English Card
How do you hedge an opinion in academic writing? *(two collocations)*
Back: **a tentative explanation** · **broadly support the view that…**
- *tentative* = given by someone not fully certain it is correct
- *The statistics **broadly support the view** that the economy is heading towards recession*
- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Hedging is a band 8 move, not a weakness</mark> — it is what lets a claim survive a counter-example

English Card
To use someone's work as your own → ______ plagiarism
Back: **commit plagiarism** — the verb for offences, like *commit a crime*.
- The prevention has its own collocation: **acknowledge your sources**
- *make plagiarism* and *do plagiarism* are both wrong
