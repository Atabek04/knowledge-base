Coding Questions
What question do examiners ask at bachelor, master's and PhD level, and what is each graded on?
Back:
- **Bachelor**: can you build? → graded on **execution**
- **Master's**: can you find out? → graded on **contribution and method**
- **PhD**: can you find out what the field did not know? → graded on **novelty the field confirmed in print**

Each step keeps the previous ability as a prerequisite and stops rewarding it.

Coding Questions
Why does "we built a system" fail a master's defence even when the system is good?
Back: It answers the **bachelor question** (can you build), which examiners stopped asking one degree ago.

- The system is evidence only when a **claim about it is being tested**
- A descriptive chapter has **no reader role**

Coding Questions
How long should the artifact chapter be in a master's dissertation?
Back: Only as long as the **evaluation needs**: which mechanisms are separately switchable, how the measurement isolates what it claims.

- Weight goes to **evaluation, results, validity threats**
- Anything past that is "a bachelor chapter in a master's binder"

Coding Questions
Why are publications the gate for a PhD specifically?
Back: "The field did not know" is a claim **about other people**, and only they can confirm it.

- Peer review at a recognised venue = the field saying **in writing** the contribution is new
- Without it, novelty is a **private** claim

Coding Questions
Kazakhstan master's defence: what are the two rule layers for publications, and which one binds?
Back:
- **ГОСО** (государственный общеобязательный стандарт образования): national **floor**, one publication or one conference talk
- **University rule** (кафедра / academic council): may raise the floor, e.g. two articles

The **stricter** (university) layer binds; plan against it.

Who decides what "published" means for a Kazakhstan master's defence, and when should you ask?
Back: The **кафедра**, stated in writing by the **учёный секретарь**.

- Never infer it from the national text
- Ask **months before** the deadline: a wrong guess costs a full submission cycle

START
Coding Questions
Предзащита vs защита: what does each test?
Back:
- **Предзащита** (pre-defence, кафедра, ~1 month before): talk rehearsal + **file check**: antiplagiat, список научных трудов signed, publication copies, documents to учебный отдел
- **Защита** (state commission): tests the **argument**; paperwork already passed

Publications are **counted at предзащита**, so they must be published by that date.

Coding Questions
The "in press" trap for a Kazakhstan defence: what is it and the safe rule?
Back: Journal accepts in March, issue appears in July, предзащита is in May → paper may **not count**.

- КОКСНВО-list journals often publish **quarterly**
- Safe reading: published = issue out with **page numbers or DOI**, not an acceptance letter
- Time submissions **backwards from предзащита** with a full review cycle of margin
