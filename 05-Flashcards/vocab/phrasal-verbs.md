A_English_Translate
To accumulate in increasing quantities because something is not being processed or removed fast enough
English: to pile up
Russian: накапливаться; скапливаться (быстрее, чем обрабатывается)
Example: If the client reads slowly, tokens **pile up** in the TCP send buffer until there is no room left.
Note: Literal: objects physically stacking up ("dishes pile up in the sink"). In networking: data accumulates in a buffer faster than it is consumed — same visual: a growing heap.
