Coding Questions
What is overfitting and how do you detect it?
Back: **Overfitting** is when a model memorizes not just the real patterns but also the **noise** in training data.
- Classic sign: high training accuracy, poor test accuracy
- The model memorized examples instead of learning generalizable patterns
- The **test set** detects this early — if performance drops on unseen data, the model is overfitting
- A large gap between training and test accuracy is a red flag
