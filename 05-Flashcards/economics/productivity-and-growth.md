Coding Questions
What is **GDP (gross domestic product)**?
Back: The total market value of all **final** goods and services produced inside a country in **one year**.

Coding Questions
A mill sells $500 of flour to a bakery, which sells $2,000 of bread. What is GDP, and why?
Back: **$2,000.** The flour's $500 is already **inside the bread's price**; adding it would **double count** it.

Coding Questions
What is the difference between a **final good** and an **intermediate good**?
Back: 
- **Final good**: bought by its **last user**
- **Intermediate good**: bought to be **used up making another good** (flour for a bakery)
- Key: GDP counts only the final one

Coding Questions
What is **double counting** in GDP?
Back: **Counting the same value twice**: once as an intermediate good, and again inside the price of the final good it went into.
- $400 tyres + $20,000 car adds **$20,000**, not $20,400

Coding Questions
What is **value added**, and how does it give the same GDP?
Back: A stage's **sales minus what it paid for intermediate goods**.
- Mill $500 + bakery ($2,000 − $500) = $2,000, the same as final sales

Coding Questions
What is **TFP (total factor productivity)**?
Back: The part of output growth **not explained by more labor or more capital**.
- It captures better methods, ideas, organization and technology
Tags: economics growth
<!--ID: 1791706130402-->
END

Coding Questions
Same 2 bakers, same oven, output rises from 1,000 to 1,300 loaves. What is the 30%?
Back: **TFP growth**: extra output from **better methods**, not more inputs.

Coding Questions
Why is TFP called a **residual**?
Back: It cannot be observed directly, so it is computed as **what is left over** after measured input growth is subtracted from output growth.
- Also called the **Solow residual**

What is Acemoglu's estimate of AI's effect on US **TFP**?
Back: **No more than about 0.66% over 10 years**; less than 0.53% after hard-to-learn tasks.
- "Nontrivial but modest" (NBER w32487)

Trap: comparing "AI adds 7% to GDP" with "AI adds 0.66% to TFP".
Back: They are **different measures**: GDP is total output, TFP is the output growth left after counting inputs.
- Compare like with like before calling one optimistic

A third baker is hired; output rises by 600 loaves, and the extra baker explains 400 of them. What is the TFP growth?
Back: **200 loaves**: the growth **left after subtracting** what the added input explains.
- TFP excludes input growth; it is never "more workers plus methods"
