What is the **equilibrium price**?
Back: The price where the **amount offered equals the amount wanted**.
- *Equilibrium* = "equal balance"

What happens when a price sits **above** equilibrium, and why does it move?
Back: A **surplus**: more offered than wanted.
- Unsold goods push sellers to **cut** the price toward equilibrium

What happens when a price sits **below** equilibrium, and why does it move?
Back: A **shortage**: more wanted than offered.
- Unserved buyers let sellers **raise** the price toward equilibrium

Coding Questions
How can technology move labor demand in **opposite directions**?
Back: It lowers demand for workers it **substitutes** for and raises demand for workers it **complements**.
- Word processors replaced typists
- Computers raised demand for IT professionals

What happened to the US **college wage premium** as information technology spread?
Back: It rose from about **30% (1980) to about 59% (2019)**.
- IT complemented high-skill workers and substituted for low-skill ones (OpenStax 4.1)

Name three things that shift **labor supply**.

- **Training required** (longer training, fewer workers)
- **Licensing and policy** (strict licences cut supply, subsidies raise it)
