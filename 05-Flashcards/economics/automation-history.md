What are the **displacement** and **reinstatement** effects?
Back: 
- **Displacement**: machines take over tasks workers did, so labor is **displaced**
- **Reinstatement**: **new tasks** humans do better are created, so labor is **reinstated**

What is **income elasticity of demand**, and why does it matter for farm automation?
Back: How much spending on a good **changes as incomes rise**.
- Food: **low**, so cheaper food meant a smaller share of income on food, and the rest went elsewhere
- Health care: **high**, so its share keeps growing

In economics, what makes a good a **necessity** versus a **luxury**?
Back: 
- **Necessity**: its spending share **falls** as income rises (food)
- **Luxury**: its share **rises** as income rises (health care, travel, education)
- Key: defined by how spending responds to income, not by glamour

What are the three links in a technology's job chain reaction?
Back: 
1. **Making** the new product (car factories)
2. **Needs the product creates** (fuel, repair, roads)
3. **How it changes life** (travel, so motels and roadside food)
- Usually the biggest gains come from links 2 and 3
