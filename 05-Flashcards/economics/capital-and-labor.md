What are the two ways people earn income from production?
Back: 
- **Labor**: working, paid as a **wage**
- **Capital**: owning productive things, paid as a **return on capital** (profit, rent, interest)

What is the **labor share**?
Back: The part of total income paid to **workers as wages**; the rest is the **capital share**.

Coding Questions
When a machine replaces a worker, where does the old wage go?
Back: It becomes **return on capital** for whoever **owns the machine** (minus the machine's running cost).
- Bakery: owner's $60k becomes $95k; labor share falls from 40% to 0%

What happened to the **global labor share** since the early 1980s, and the main cause found?
Back: It **fell** in most countries and industries.
- Karabarbounis and Neiman: **cheaper investment goods** (machines, computers) explain about **half**
- Firms swapped workers for cheaper capital
