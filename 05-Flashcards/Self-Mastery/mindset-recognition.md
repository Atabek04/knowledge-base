## Sociometer Theory

START
Coding Questions
What is Sociometer Theory and who proposed it?
Back:
Mark Leary (1995) — self-esteem is not a measure of how good you feel about yourself. It is a **real-time psychological gauge** tracking your perceived standing in the eyes of others.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
According to Sociometer Theory, what triggers anxiety and recognition-seeking behavior?
Back:
When the sociometer **drops** — through rejection, exclusion, or being ignored — the mind fires anxiety and pushes behavior toward regaining social acceptance.

Recognition, fame, and projecting strength are all attempts to keep the gauge charged.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What did Leary & Baumeister (2000) find about what self-esteem actually tracks?
Back:
Self-esteem tracks **social inclusion and exclusion** more reliably than actual competence or objective achievement.

Threats to belonging triggered behavioral responses comparable to physical threats.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the Islamic reframe of Sociometer Theory — why is the sociometer not corrupt in itself?
Back:
Humans are social by **fitrah** — created to care about belonging. The sociometer isn't corrupt.

The problem is **direction**: pointed at creation's opinion (unstable, shifting) → empties constantly. Pointed at Allah's pleasure (fixed, eternal) → stabilizes.

Ibn al-Qayyim: the heart enslaved to people's approval can never rest — it chases something that never stays still.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the practical application of Sociometer Theory when you feel the pull toward recognition?
Back:
Pause and ask: **whose approval is this meter currently tracking?**

Not to suppress the desire — but to identify the direction. Then deliberately reorient: the action, the intention, the audience.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

## Contingent Self-Worth

START
Coding Questions
What is contingent self-worth?
Back:
A structural dependency on external outcomes — approval, performance, how others perceive you — to maintain inner stability. (Crocker & Park, 2004)

Every interaction becomes a test. Every recognition dose satisfies briefly, then the gauge empties again.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What was the counterintuitive finding of Crocker & Park (2004) about contingent self-worth?
Back:
People with **high contingent self-worth** in domains like others' approval showed:
- Higher anxiety
- More depression
- More conflict
- **Worse** academic and social performance

Getting more of what they chased made outcomes **worse**, not better.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
Why does fame or recognition never "fix" the recognition hunger, according to Contingent Self-Worth theory?
Back:
Because the dependency is **structural**, not a quantity problem. The system needs continuous input to stay charged — no single dose permanently satisfies a contingent source.

More recognition feeds the dependency; it doesn't remove it.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What Islamic concept maps directly to the psychology of contingent self-worth?
Back:
The hadith: *"The son of Adam will not be satisfied even if he had a valley full of gold."*

The **nafs al-ammara** always escalates its demands. No created thing can fill what only Allah's recognition can fill. Islam named this centuries before Crocker & Park (2004).
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the diagnostic test for high contingent self-worth?
Back:
After a recognition moment (compliment, win, someone impressed) — **how long does the relief last?**

Short duration = high contingency. The fix is not more achievement — it's relocating the source of worth to something stable.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

## Riya

START
Coding Questions
What is riya, according to Ibn al-Qayyim?
Back:
The gradual **reorientation of the self** — actions, speech, appearance, even worship — around what others will think.

Ibn al-Qayyim calls it ***shirk al-khafi*** (hidden association) in *Madarij al-Salikin*: placing creation's opinion alongside Allah's in a position it was never meant to occupy.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What are Ibn al-Qayyim's three grades of riya?
Back:
1. Doing good **for** people's praise
2. Doing good **better** when people are watching
3. **Abandoning** good when no one is watching

Most people live in grade 2 or 3 without recognizing it as riya.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the internal test for detecting riya in an action?
Back:
Ask: **"Would I do this exactly the same if no one ever found out?"**

If the answer is no — the action has riya embedded in it.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What modern psychology concept maps to riya, and why is it costly?
Back:
**Introjected regulation** (Deci & Ryan, Self-Determination Theory, 1985) — doing things to avoid shame or gain approval rather than from genuine values.

Identified as the most psychologically costly form of motivation: high effort, high anxiety, low satisfaction.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is Ibn al-Qayyim's prescribed cure for riya?
Back:
***Ikhlas*** — active sincerity. Not suppression of the desire to be seen, but **deliberate reorientation** of the deed toward Allah before, during, and after.

Ikhlas is practiced deed by deed — not a feeling you wait for.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What du'a did the Prophet ﷺ teach specifically for hidden shirk (riya)?
Back:
*Allahumma inni a'udhu bika an ushrika bika wa ana a'lam*

**"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from knowingly associating partners with You."**

Recite before actions where recognition-seeking may be present.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

## Terror Management Theory

START
Coding Questions
What is Terror Management Theory and who proposed it?
Back:
Greenberg, Solomon & Pyszczynski (1986) — humans are uniquely aware of their mortality, which creates **baseline existential terror** that the mind manages unconsciously through **symbolic immortality**: believing that fame, legacy, or cultural impact allows you to persist after death.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is symbolic immortality in Terror Management Theory?
Back:
The belief that **being known and remembered** allows a person to persist beyond physical death.

It's why fame-seeking feels categorically different from ordinary approval: approval satisfies the sociometer; fame promises you won't be **erased from existence**.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What did TMT research find when subjects were primed with thoughts of death (mortality salience)?
Back:
Subjects showed significantly **increased desire for fame, status, and cultural legacy**.

The effect replicated across cultures. Fame-seeking spikes precisely when death anxiety activates — even subconsciously.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
Why does western psychology (TMT) hit its ceiling on the fame-seeking problem?
Back:
TMT correctly identifies the terror — mortality — but prescribes only **secular buffers**: legacy, being remembered, cultural impact.

These delay the terror; they don't dissolve it. Every person who achieved fame still died. Every legacy eventually fades.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the Islamic answer to the existential terror that drives fame-seeking (per Terror Management Theory)?
Back:
The **akhira** dissolves the need for symbolic immortality. If the next life is real and eternal, being forgotten by people is irrelevant.

Ibn al-Qayyim: genuine belief in the akhira frees the heart from the slavery of reputation — the only audience that matters outlasts all human memory.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END

START
Coding Questions
What honest question should you ask when the pull toward fame activates?
Back:
**"What am I afraid of losing if no one remembers me?"**

The honest answer is almost always: *significance beyond death.*

That fear has a real answer — not a better personal brand. It is stronger **iman** in what comes after.
Tags: self-mastery mindset recognition
END
