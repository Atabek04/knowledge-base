## Dopamine: Wanting vs Liking

START
Coding Questions
What is the difference between "wanting" and "liking" in Berridge-Robinson's incentive-sensitization theory?
Back:
- **Wanting** — encoded by mesolimbic dopamine (VTA, nucleus accumbens). Motivational pull: the urgent drive to *pursue*.
- **Liking** — encoded by opioid circuits. Hedonic pleasure: the actual experience of satisfaction.

These are **separate systems** that can decouple — a person can compulsively want something they no longer enjoy.
Tags: self-mastery discipline neuroscience
END

START
Coding Questions
Why does telling yourself "I know I won't enjoy it" fail to stop a craving?
Back:
Craving (wanting) and pleasure (liking) are different neural circuits.

The wanting circuit fires in response to **cues**, not rational evaluation of enjoyment. Addressing one system does not address the other. The craving has already fired before the judgment lands.
Tags: self-mastery discipline neuroscience
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the Islamic parallel to dopamine incentive sensitization?
Back:
Ibn al-Qayyim (*Ighathat al-Lahfan*) describes how **shahwa** (desire-pull) and **ladhdha** (pleasure) diverge under repeated sin.

The nafs ammārah learns to *want* without needing to *receive* — the pull is trained, the satisfaction is not. What neuroscience calls incentive sensitization, Ibn al-Qayyim calls the trained nafs.

## Dual-Systems Brain Conflict

START
Coding Questions
What are the two competing neural systems in dual-systems theory of self-control?
Back:
| System | Location | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Impulsive** | Mesolimbic / striatum | Fast, automatic, dopamine-driven |
| **Deliberative** | Lateral prefrontal cortex | Slow, effortful, rational |

Self-control fails when the impulsive system dominates — which happens when the deliberative system is **weakened**, not when knowledge disappears.
Tags: self-mastery discipline neuroscience
END

START
Coding Questions
What is iRISA and what does it explain about addiction?
Back:
**Impaired Response Inhibition and Salience Attribution** (Goldstein & Volkow, 2011).

In addictive disorders, the inhibition circuitry becomes neurobiologically compromised. The person's declarative knowledge ("I know this is destroying me") remains intact — but the circuit that converts that knowledge into **stopped behavior** is impaired.

The disconnect is structural, not informational. This is why "just decide to stop" fails for severe addiction.

## Ibtilā' and Human Agency

START
Coding Questions
What are the four things that collapse if humans have no real ikhtiyār (genuine choice)?
Back:
1. **Ibtilā' becomes theatre** — a test with a predetermined outcome is not a test
2. **Darajāt in Jannah become meaningless** — proportional reward requires graduated real merit
3. **Tawbah becomes impossible** — a robot that malfunctions doesn't repent, it gets repaired
4. **The structure of jazā' collapses** — reward and punishment presuppose culpable choice

END

START
Coding Questions
What is the divine test (ibtilā') actually testing?
Back:
Not whether you can live without a nafs that pulls toward dunya.

Allah created the nafs ammārah, the shahwa, and the pull of the immediate. The test is:

> **Given this nafs, given the knowledge, given the Messenger — what will you do?**

The difficulty is built into the design. A test with no difficulty tests nothing.

## Irāda Kawniyya vs Irāda Sharī'iyya

START
Coding Questions
What is the difference between irāda kawniyya and irāda sharī'iyya?
Back:
- **Irāda kawniyya** (ontological will / decree) — what Allah *permits to occur*. Everything that exists, including sins and kufr, occurs through kawnī permission. Without it, nothing can occur.
- **Irāda sharī'iyya** (legislative will / command) — what Allah *loves, commands, and is pleased with*. This is the standard of accountability.

Kufr occurs by kawnī permission — yet Allah does not love kufr (Q. 39:7). **Accountability attaches to the sharī'ī will, not the kawnī permission.**
Tags: self-mastery discipline islamic aqeedah
END

START
Coding Questions
What did Ibn Taymiyyah say about using qadar as an excuse for sin?
Back:
> "Whoever affirms Divine Decree but uses it to **cancel commandments and prohibitions** is worse than one who denies qadar altogether."

Why worse? The denier of qadar maintains a coherent framework of accountability. The one who weaponizes qadar has used a true premise (Allah's sovereignty) to neutralize the entire purpose of revelation.
Tags: self-mastery discipline islamic aqeedah
END

START
Coding Questions
What is Ibn al-Qayyim's personal challenge to the person who uses qadar as an excuse?
Back:
> "If your destiny were an argument for usurping your Lord's rights, O ignorant one — would it not be an excuse for your wife or your son?"

You never accept qadar from someone who wrongs *you*. You invoke it only toward Allah — exposing it as self-serving rationalization, not theology.

## Taklīf Conditions and Nafs Weakness

START
Coding Questions
What are the three conditions of taklīf (Islamic religious accountability)?
Back:
1. **'Aql** — functional reason; capacity to understand the divine command
2. **Bulūgh** — maturity; "the pen is lifted from three: the sleeping, the child, the insane"
3. **Balāgh** — the message reached them; "We never punish until We send a messenger" (Q. 17:15)

Da'f al-nafs (weakness of will) is **not** on this list.
Tags: self-mastery discipline islamic
END

START
Coding Questions
What is the usūl al-fiqh distinction between 'ajz haqīqī and da'f al-irāda?
Back:
- **'Ajz haqīqī** (true inability) — the person literally *cannot* perform → taklīf is lifted
- **Da'f al-irāda** (weakness of will) — the person *can* but finds it hard → taklīf **remains**

Q. 2:286 ("Allah does not burden beyond capacity") addresses 'ajz haqīqī — not the difficulty of resisting temptation.
Tags: self-mastery discipline islamic
END

START
Coding Questions
What is suqūt al-taklīf and why is it an error?
Back:
The claim that weakness of the nafs *removes* religious obligation.

Ibn al-Qayyim devotes major sections of *Madarij al-Sālikīn* to refuting it. Da'f al-nafs is precisely **what the Sharī'ah was sent to address** — not exempt from it.

Contrast with **muhāsaba** (honest acknowledgment of weakness + repentance) — which is virtuous and the foundation of the spiritual path.
Tags: self-mastery discipline islamic
END

## Ghaflah: Chosen Heedlessness

START
Coding Questions
What is Ibn al-Qayyim's exact distinction between ghaflah and nisyān? (Source: Madarij al-Sālikīn)
Back:
> **"الغفلة ترك باختيار الغافل، والنسيان ترك بغير اختياره"**
>
> "Ghaflah is leaving by the **choice** of the heedless one. Nisyān is leaving without his choice."

- Ghaflah = voluntary turning-away → blameworthy, taklīf holds
- Nisyān = involuntary forgetting → excused, falls outside taklīf

Qur'anic proof: Allah commands "وَلَا تَكُن مِّنَ الْغَافِلِين" (7:205) but never commands against forgetting — because you cannot be commanded against what you cannot control.
Tags: self-mastery islamic discipline
END

START
Coding Questions
What happens to iman at the moment of sin, according to Ibn Abbas (Bukhari #6809)?
Back:
Ikrimah asked Ibn Abbas how iman is taken from the sinner. Ibn Abbas interlaced his fingers, then separated them:

> **"هَكَذَا وَشَبَّكَ بَيْنَ أَصَابِعِهِ ثُمَّ أَخْرَجَهَا. فَإِنْ تَابَ عَادَ إِلَيْهِ هَكَذَا"**

The Sunni position (Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyyah): what separates is the **light and taste of iman** (نور الإيمان), not iman's root. The person does not become a kafir — but the experiential weight of iman dims, leaving shahwa without a counterweight.

Ibn al-Qayyim (Ighathat al-Lahfan): "العلم نور يقذفه الله في القلب، والمعصية تطفئ ذلك النور."
Tags: self-mastery islamic aqeedah
END

START
Coding Questions
How does ghaflah connect to present bias and hyperbolic discounting?
Back:
Ghaflah is the **spiritual mechanism** by which present bias becomes possible for a believer who knows better.

- The akhirah is not forgotten — it is **known but experientially weightless** (ghaflah has made it abstract)
- The dunya is immediately vivid and felt
- The result: the same asymmetry as hyperbolic discounting — the abstract future loses to the felt present

The fix in both frameworks: **dhikr** (Islamic) and **commitment devices** (behavioral) both work by restoring felt weight before temptation arrives, not during it.

## 'Ilm + Irāda: Knowledge Without Will

START
Coding Questions
What are Ibn Taymiyyah's three conditions for voluntary action? (Majmu' Fatawa, Vol. 8)
Back:
Every voluntary action requires all three simultaneously:

| Condition | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Intent / Knowledge | قصد / علم | Knowing what the action is |
| **Will** | **إرادة** | Genuinely wanting to do it |
| Capacity | قدرة | Having the ability |

If any one is absent, the voluntary action does not occur. Most people who fail to act on what they know are **not missing knowledge** — they are missing irāda (will).

Adding more information does not fix an irāda deficit.
Tags: self-mastery islamic discipline
END

START
Coding Questions
What are Ibn al-Qayyim's three levels of yaqīn and why do they matter for sin?
Back:
1. **'Ilm al-yaqīn** — inferential certainty: you know about it (heard/studied). Most believers' relationship with the akhirah.
2. **'Ayn al-yaqīn** — visual certainty: you've seen/felt it as real.
3. **Ḥaqq al-yaqīn** — experiential certainty: direct encounter. You don't argue yourself away from fire after being burned.

**The problem:** Dunya pulls at ḥaqq al-yaqīn (felt immediately). Akhirah sits at 'ilm al-yaqīn (known abstractly). The fight is structurally unequal.

**The solution:** Move the akhirah from 'ilm al-yaqīn toward 'ayn al-yaqīn through tafakkur al-mawt, Qur'anic reflection on Jannah/Jahannam, suhba, and consistent dhikr.
Tags: self-mastery islamic discipline
END

START
Coding Questions
What does the Arabic root عَقَلَ (aqala) reveal about true 'aql vs. mere information?
Back:
عَقَلَ = to **bind, tether, restrain**. A tethered camel is mu'aqqal.

True 'aql is not just having information — it is knowledge **tethered to the heart** so it produces behavior.

A person can have 'ilm (data in the head) without 'aql (bound comprehension). They can explain the truth, recite it, teach it — but it does not move them because it is untethered.

The question is not "do you know?" — it is "**has this knowledge become binding upon your heart?**"
Tags: self-mastery islamic
END

## The Purpose Frame: Al-Ra'd 13:11

START
Coding Questions
What three things does Al-Ra'd 13:11 presuppose about human agency?
Back:
> "إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُوا مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ"
>
> "Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves."

1. **Real agency** — *they* must do the changing, not have it done to them
2. **Process** — sustained effort, not one decision
3. **Divine partnership** — you change first, then Allah changes your condition. The order matters.

Counter to both despair ("my nafs is too strong to change") and passivity ("everything is from Allah anyway").
Tags: self-mastery islamic aqeedah
END
