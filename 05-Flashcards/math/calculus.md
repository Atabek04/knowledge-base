Coding Questions
What is a derivative and what does it measure?
Back: The **instantaneous rate of change** of a function at a specific point. It answers "how fast is this changing right now?" Found by making Δx approach zero in the slope formula m = Δy/Δx. For y = x², the derivative is 2x — giving the slope at any point x.

Coding Questions
What is slope and how does it differ between lines and curves?
Back:
- **Slope** = how much y changes when x changes: m = Δy/Δx
- **Lines** — slope is constant everywhere. Predictions using slope are **exact**.
- **Curves** — slope changes at every point. Predictions using slope are **approximate** (only accurate for small Δx).
- In ML, slope is also called **weight** — same idea, different name.

What is a tangent line and how does it relate to the derivative?
Back: A tangent line **touches the curve at exactly one point**. Its slope equals the derivative at that point. Three ways to say the same thing: "derivative at x=1 is 2" = "slope of tangent line is 2" = "rate of change at x=1 is 2."

Coding Questions
What are the two methods to find the slope of a curve at a point?
Back:
- **Method 1: Calculate derivative** — use when you have the equation. Apply derivative rules, plug in x. Gives exact answer.
- **Method 2: Draw tangent line visually** — use when you only have a graph. Draw tangent by eye, measure rise/run. Gives approximate answer.
