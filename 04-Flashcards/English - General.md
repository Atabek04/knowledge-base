# English - General Vocabulary

## Describing Situations

contemporary:::existing or happening **now**

unprecedented:::never having **happened or existed** in the past

pervasive:::present or noticeable in **every part** of a thing or place

backdrop:::**background situation or context** in which something happens

## Collaboration & Progress

synergy:::combined power of a group **greater than** the sum of individual efforts

convergence:::two or more things **becoming similar** or coming together

innovation:::a **new idea or method**

momentum:::the **force that keeps** an object (or idea) moving forward

traction:::gaining **acceptance and adoption** (figurative: like a tire gripping to move forward)

## Physical Actions (with Figurative Meanings)

grip:::to hold **very tightly**

tap:::to **access or utilize** something that already exists (like tapping a water line)

supplement:::to **add something** to make it larger or better

## Age Groups

child:::person between **birth and puberty** (under 14 years)

teen/teenager:::person between **13 and 19** (linguistic term based on "-teen" suffix)

youngster:::informal term for young person (**not used** in formal/medical contexts)
