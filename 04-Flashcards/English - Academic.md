# English - Academic Vocabulary

## Research Methodology

meta-analysis:::statistical method that **combines results** from multiple studies to find overall patterns

systematic review:::research that **identifies, evaluates, and synthesizes** all relevant studies on a topic using explicit methods

empirical study:::research based on **observed/measured data** rather than theory alone

non-empirical:::research based on **theory, logic, or existing literature** rather than new data collection

## Academic Writing

seminal:::containing **important new ideas** and having great influence on later work

synthesis:::mixing of **different ideas or influences** to make a whole that is new

substantial:::large in **size, value, or importance**

conceptual:::based on **ideas or principles**

## Psychology & Development

cognitive:::connected with **thinking** or conscious mental processes

cognitive overload:::mental exhaustion when **demands exceed working memory capacity**

fragmented attention:::**splitting focus** between activities, causing mental fatigue from context-switching

adolescent:::individual **between 10 and 19** (developmental/biological term covering puberty and brain maturation)

## Actions & Changes

intervention:::intentional action to **change a situation** with aim of improving it

consolidate:::to become or cause something to become **stronger and more certain**

undermine:::to make someone **less confident or powerful**, often gradually

proliferation:::something **increasing a lot and suddenly** in number or amount

emerge:::to **appear** or come into existence
