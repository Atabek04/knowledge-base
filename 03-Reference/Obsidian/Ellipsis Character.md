## Difference

- `...` (three dots) — three separate period characters
- `…` (ellipsis) — single Unicode character, proper typography

Ellipsis doesn't break awkwardly across lines and is the correct typographical standard.

## How to Type

**Windows:** `Alt + 0133` (numeric keypad)

**Custom shortcut:** Use AutoHotKey to bind `Alt + .`
